# R4 One-point extensions of block-circulant 20-point colourings

**Pattern.** P3. **Facts used.** none. **Scope.** every valid
block-circulant colouring of K₂₀ over ℤ₁₀ with 2 rings (all 40, up to the
1 ↔ 2 swap), and the first 100,000 in backtracking order over ℤ₅ with 4
rings and over ℤ₄ with 5 rings; each seed's one-point extensions found
exactly by `extensions_with_depth`.
**Command.** `cargo run --release -p ramsey-k3-k4e-k4e --bin r4_extend_block -- <m> <rings> <limit> --out problems/ramsey-k3-k4e-k4e/scratch/r4/answer_<m>_<rings>.txt` for (10, 2, 100000), (5, 4, 100000), (4, 5, 100000) · **Commit.** f348c38 · **Date.** 2026-10-30
**Validated.** Seeds come from R2's engine (validated there, moved
unchanged into `block.rs`: identical counts and node numbers);
`extensions` is validated in `cargo test` (O1: none; O1 minus a point:
two). The binary rescores every extension it reports with `violations`;
at K₁₅ it finds extendable seeds (392 of the first 2,000).
**Machine, Runtime (runs over a minute).** Ryzen 7 3700X · ℤ₁₀: 0.2 s; ℤ₅ with 4 rings stopped by the user after 36 min, before its first report
**Checked.** unchecked

**Outcome.** ℤ₁₀ with 2 rings, complete: none of the 40 seeds extends; the
deepest partial row colours 19 of 20 pairs. ℤ₅ with 4 rings and ℤ₄ with 5
rings: inconclusive, stopped without output (the binary reports only at the end).
