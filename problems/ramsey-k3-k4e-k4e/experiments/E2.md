# E2 Every block-circulant colouring of K₂₁ over ℤ₇

**Tests.** I2. **Uses.** none (colours 1 and 2 are swapped away:
the first class not coloured 0 takes colour 1, which loses no answer up to
that swap). **Scope.** all 3³⁰ assignments of colours to I2's 30 classes,
by backtracking: a class's colour is refused when a triangle or 4-point set
through a point of ring offset 0 whose classes are all assigned breaks a
rule (every ℤ₇-orbit of such sets has a member through such a point).
**Command.** `cargo run --release -p ramsey-k3-k4e-k4e --bin r2_block_circulant -- 7 3 --out problems/ramsey-k3-k4e-k4e/scratch/r2/first.txt` · **Commit.** 3bc35fd · **Date.** 2026-10-30
**Validated.** Its counts equal an enumeration of every assignment scored
on the full colouring by `violations` (`--brute`): 7,470 at m = 3, 3 rings;
601,800 at m = 4, 3 rings; 1,311 at m = 5, 2 rings. Every colouring it
reports is rescored by `violations` before it is written.
**Machine, Runtime (runs over a minute).** Ryzen 7 3700X · 5.7 s
**Checked.** Refuter: SURVIVES (2026-10-30). Its own search (shift orbits, no class formula, no anchoring, no colour break, two search orders) finds 0 valid and exactly twice the engine's count at nine smaller sizes; a SAT encoding of the literal rules plus shift invariance, solved by CaDiCaL 2.1.2, is UNSAT (`scratch/refuter/r2r3/`).

**Outcome.** exhausted: no valid assignment (7,094,660 nodes). For
comparison, the same engine finds 727,900 valid block-circulants of K₁₅
(m = 5) and 617,760 of K₁₈ (m = 6), where no circulant is valid. Every
permutation of order 7 moving all 21 points is conjugate to this shift, so
no valid colouring of K₂₁ keeps its colours under one.
