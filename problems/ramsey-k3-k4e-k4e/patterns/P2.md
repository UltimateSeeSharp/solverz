# P2 Block-circulant over ℤ₇

Three rings of 7 points, point (r, i) for ring r and i ∈ ℤ₇; the pair
(r, i)(s, j) has a colour depending on r, s and j − i (with i − j for r = s
identified). 3 rings × 3 inner distances + 3 ring pairs × 7 differences =
30 classes: 3³⁰ ≈ 2·10¹⁴ colourings. Contains P1 (ℤ₂₁ = ℤ₇ × ℤ₃).

**Kill test.** Local search minimizing `violations` over the 30 classes;
if it gets close, an exhaustive or SAT run over the 30 classes settles P2.
**Status.** new.
