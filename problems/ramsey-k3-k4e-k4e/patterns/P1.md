# P1 Circulant on ℤ₂₁

Points 0..20 on a circle; the pair ij has the colour of its distance
min(|i − j|, 21 − |i − j|) ∈ 1..10. Symmetric constructions give most
small Ramsey lower bounds, and this is the smallest such space:
3¹⁰ = 59,049 colourings.

**Kill test.** Score all 59,049 with `violations`: one at 0 solves the
problem; none exhausts P1, and the best near-misses show which distances fail.
**Status.** new.
