# C77 Exact-sum lemmas

**Statement.** (a) If d ∈ R and d ≥ every number of R: V(R, d) ≠ loss. (b) If A ⊆ S has positive numbers, sum(A) = d, c ∈ A, and for every w ∈ S ∖ A with w > c, V((S ∖ A) ∖ {w}, w) ≠ loss (opening w of the tied S ∖ A does not win): V(S, d) ≠ loss. (c) If A ⊆ R is nonempty with positive numbers, sum(A) = d and V(R, d) = loss: some w ∈ R ∖ A with w > max A wins as an opening of the tied R ∖ A.
**Use.** A block summing exactly to the deficit can be taken to reach a tie, so a loss forces a winning opening above the block; the engine of C73.
