# C102 Copying repaired with a score debt ≤ 2

**Statement.** C64's family: N ≥ 11 with N(N + 1)/2 even, 3 ≤ b ≤ N, R = R_b = I_N ∖ {1, b−1, b}, y−1, y, y+1 ∈ R, U = (R ∖ {y−1, y}, 1), V = (R ∖ {y, y+1}, 1). (a) One-pair no-jump: never U = loss with V = win. (b) Two-buffer translator: whenever V is a mover win, a causal protocol couples U to V's winning strategy (`CatchUp.TwoBuffer.Protocol`, `CatchUp/TwoBufferProtocol.lean`; protected player: the mover; the controller commits to one game before each move; every opponent move in U and every win-keeping protected reply in V is covered) such that at every step each game has at most 2 numbers the other lacks, and at the end completedMargin(U) ≥ completedMargin(V) − 2 (score debt ≤ 2).

**Use.** (b) ⇒ (a) on paper: U and V have even effective totals (`adjacent_hole_effective_totals_even`, `ScoreDebtRepairCertificates.lean`), so at the end the won V has completed margin ≥ 2, U ≥ 0, and U's end is no loss (terminal step verified: `completed_margin_transfer`, `Earlier/CompletedMargin.lean`, C33). Their caveat: Lean has no general extraction from a Protocol to an actual strategy. (a) suffices for C74's interior hypothesis: a winning opening y of R_b with both neighbors gives U = L and V = W (`after_opening_loss_forces_neighbor_value_jump`, `CatchUp/Earlier/DeficitWinningBound.lean`), which (a) forbids; by C103 only y ≥ b + 2 matter. (a) is weaker than C81 at j = 2 (U ≠ loss outright). Their CU-L022 target, which C64 quotes as open.
**Evidence.** (a): 0 jumps in their 790 triples at N = 11, 12, 15, 16, 19, 20 (historical; neither neighbor ever loses); mine (2026-11-05, `position_value`) replays N = 11, 12 (42 and 56 triples): V wins only at (N, b, y) = (11, 3, 7), (11, 4, 8), (11, 6, 3), (11, 9, 3), (12, 5, 8), (12, 9, 4), U draws there; U wins in 8 others. C81's census (U never loses at even-total N ≤ 28) gives (a) there; V never wins from N = 15 (their table to 20; C81's two-sided census: draws for j ≤ 3 from N = 19, to 28), so (b) has content only at N = 11, 12 so far.
(b), verified in our build on one instance (ported 2026-11-04, standard axioms, `decide +kernel`): C64's (11, 4, 8), U = ({2,5,6,9,10,11}, 1), V = ({2,5,6,7,10,11}, 1):
- `CatchUp.TwoBuffer.mandatory_protocol` (`TwoBufferMandatory.lean`, via `mandatory_policy_checked` and `run_check_sound`): a Protocol U → V (their CU-L026, 87-node policy).
- `CatchUp.TwoBuffer.mandatory_scalar_bounds` (`CompletedMarginBounds.lean`): the protected player can force completed margin ≥ 0 in U and ≥ 2 in V, the opponent ≤ 0 and ≤ 2: margins exactly 0 and 2, so debt 2 is tight here. `protocol_necessary_bound`: any Protocol with U capped at a by the opponent and V forced to b > 0 needs b − 2 ≤ a.
- `CatchUp.ScoreDebtRepair.repaired_margin_bound`, `actual_can_avoid_loss` (`Earlier/ScoreDebtTerminalRepair.lean`, CU-L024), every k: from ({k+4, 2k+5, 2k+6}, k+5) with the opponent to move, taking the largest number on protected turns ends at completed margin ≥ that of the shadow ({k+4, 2k+6}, k+4) (margin 2) minus 2; a three-number terminal gadget, intermediate debt can exceed 2.
- `CatchUp.TwoBuffer` in `TwoPickDebtRepair.lean` (CU-L027), every k: from ({k+3, k+4, 2k+5, 2k+6}, 2k+6) the turn k+3, 2k+5 leaves ({k+4, 2k+6}, k+2) (`preparation_turn`); the shadow's only win-keeping pick from ({k+4, 2k+6, 2k+7}, k+3) is 2k+7 (`bridge_forced_parametric`) and reaches the same pair at deficit k+4; copying both picks of the pair then keeps U ≠ loss with margin gap exactly 2 (`copied_pair_repair`, `repaired_pair_all_choices`); U's prefix 2, 11 reaches the k = 2 root (`mandatory_eleven_preparation`).
**Tried.** Plain copying fails (C64), and no-jump does not extend to many deleted pairs (C65). Two stricter debt policies fail (CU-L025, `ScoreDebtRepairCertificates.lean`, verified): copying the common 6 at the repair entry ends at margins 2 (actual) and 10 (shadow), breaking the 2-point bound (`copied_suffix_margin_obstruction`); "take 2, then always the largest" from U ends at margin −2 (`global_max_policy_obstruction`). Their caveats: one finite instance, no parametric translator, no extraction theorem; eagerly erasing an early-terminal remainder invalidates the policy.
**Proof.** Open.
