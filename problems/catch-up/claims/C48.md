# C48 The mover at a tie never loses the rounds game

**Evidence.** (journal 2026-10-26: rounds game tables to 24 offsets): R({0,4,5,6,7,8,10,11}, 0) = loss (best margin −1); 22,992 of the 4.2M even sets within {0..23}, none within {0..10}. Rules out: an induction over all offset sets through "the first picker at a tie is safe" or "the mover never ends worse than its debt" (μ(O, d) ≥ −d); a proof of T1 or T2 must use the structure of intervals or pair sets. In the real game the same sets are player-2 wins once placed high, e.g. {75,80,81,84,86,88,89,91} (even total 674, every opening loses; refuter's find, re-checked with `set_value` and `reference_value`), so even-total sets of distinct numbers can lose for player 1.
