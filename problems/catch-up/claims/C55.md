# C55 One strategy for all heights

**Evidence.** (log 2026-10-26: no drawing strategy works across all heights): on B(h, p) with h unknown to the players (only play reveals it), no player has one drawing strategy for every real h once p ≥ 3 (P1, p odd) or p ≥ 4; the fewest h-ranges grow with p (P1: 2, 2, 3, 4, 5 for p = 3..7), split at integers. Rules out: proving C32 or C12 with one strategy uniform in the height. Correction (refuter, 2026-10-28, log: TD's data survives the refuter): the split points depend on the direction of the greedy search (p = 3, P1 searched downward gives (0, 2), [2, ∞)), so "split at integers" and "must know the integer band" read too much into them; the counts stand.
