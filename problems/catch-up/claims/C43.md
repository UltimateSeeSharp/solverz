# C43 Fixed hole bounds under drawing play

**Evidence.** (journal 2026-10-22: holes grow under drawing play): for holes below max, internal holes, broken adjacent pairs and broken mirror pairs, and every fixed bound h, the attacker can force more than h holes at a defender's turn start with c(h) numbers left, c(h) growing with N (adjacent pairs, h = 2: 0, 6, 8, 11 left at N = 12, 16, 20, 24). Rules out: an I2 class "at most h holes" with fixed h plus a bounded endgame.
