# C120 The draw for every stopping threshold

For every integer θ ≥ 0 there is an N₀(θ) such that the threshold-θ variant of Catch-Up (a turn takes at least one number and continues while the mover's total is below the opponent's + θ) on {1..N} is a draw at every even-total N ≥ N₀(θ). C1 is θ = 0 with N₀ = 3.

**Use.** I34. A generalization of C1 across turn rules; a proof for every θ at once cannot use the level law (false from θ = 2, I31) or the exact tie rule.
**Evidence.** [E46](../experiments/E46.md) (committed `Threshold`): θ = 0..10, every even total N ≤ 23; N₀ = 3, 3, 4, 11, 15, 12 for θ = 0..5, and θ = 6..10 not yet settled by N = 23 (non-draws ±2 at the largest N). Scratch (`scratch/a19/variant/var.rs`, `vararr.rs`, I31): every even total N ≤ 15 draws for θ = 0, 1 (and θ = −1); θ = 2 from N = 4, θ = 3 from N = 11, θ = 4 from N = 15, θ = 5 at N = 16, 19, 20; θ = 6 loses at N = 16, 20 and draws at 19, 23; θ = 7 is +2 through N = 20 and draws at 23; θ = 8 is a non-draw through N = 23. Array engine (`vararr.rs`, N = 15..23, θ = 5..10): θ = 5 draws at every even total 15..23; θ = 6 draws at N = 19, 23 and loses at 16, 20 (−2); θ = 7 draws first at N = 23; θ = 8, 9, 10 are non-draws through N = 23 with shrinking margins (θ = 10: −6, 4, −4, −2, 2 at N = 15, 16, 19, 20, 23). N ≡ 3 mod 4 draws before N ≡ 0 mod 4; N₀(θ) grows faster than linearly (about 3, 11, 15, ≤ 15, ≥ 24, ≥ 24 for θ = 2..7).
