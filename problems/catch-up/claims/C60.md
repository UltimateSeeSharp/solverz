# C60 Pairing bound

**Statement.** S a finite multiset of positive integers, d ≥ 0, m the margin game (C33). Sort V = S ⊎ {d} decreasingly, v1 ≥ … ≥ vn, and set v_{n+1} = 0. P(V) = max over odd j ≤ n of (v_j − v_{j+1}): the largest gap with an odd number of elements of V above it; equivalently, pair V from the top (the last element with 0 if n is odd) and take the worst pair difference. PG: m(S, d) ≥ −P(S ⊎ {d}).
**Use.** P ≤ γ, so C60 ⇒ C37 ⇒ C1 by C37's reductions; directly, P({1..N} ⊎ {0}) = 1. P = 0 (exact twins) is the twins fact. Facts (mine, paper): P(V) = max(v1 − v2, P(V − {v1, v2})); P(W ⊎ {0}) = P(W); replacing the deficit and its partner in the top-down pairing by their difference never increases P.

**Evidence.** [E20](../experiments/E20.md): 0 violations on every S ⊆ {1..22}, d ≤ 22, and on multisets over {1..10} (multiplicity ≤ 2, d ≤ 30); sharper than C37 at 38% of positions.
**Tried.** (2026-10-30, `scratch/a13/`) No local proof from itself: with k rounds of look-ahead and C60 at every smaller position, the bound certifies itself on all S ⊆ {1..15}, d < 32 only from k = 6; the last failures are intervals ({1..12} needs 5 rounds, {1..14} at d = 1 needs 6), so the depth grows with N (`lookahead.rs`; k = 1 fails already at S = {2, 3, 4}, d = 1, where hand-overs need win amounts). Exact values for endgames of ≤ k numbers do not help: failures stay at intervals and grow with the range (`potodd.rs`). O21's step fails for P too: after every opening of {1..N}, even total N ≤ 16, some reply leaves the opener P = 2 (`step.rs`).
Case split by the top two a ≥ b of S: d ≥ a follows by induction (take a; the merge keeps P); b ≤ d < a follows from NPd, m(S, d) ≥ min(−m(S − a, 0), −(a − d)), 0 failures in 524,268 cases within {1..18} (unproved), and C60 at ties of smaller sets; a = b > d is the twins fact. The core a > b > d contains every interval at a tie; there taking a or b meets the bound at 91% of positions, never at intervals (`peel.rs`, `npd.rs`, `corec.rs`). A value-level peel of the top pair, m(S, d) ≥ min(m(S − {a, b}, d), −(a − b)), fails 3,316 times within {1..18} (S = {5, 6, 8, 9}, d = 2: m = −2 = −P).
**Proof.** Open.
