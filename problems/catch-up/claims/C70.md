# C70 Small numbers guard non-loss

**Statement.** (a) R with 2 ∈ R, every element ≥ 2 and sum(R) odd: V(R, 1) ≠ loss. (b) S with every element ≥ 3, d ≥ 2, d + sum(S) even, d ≤ sum(S) and d − 1 ∈ S: V(S, d) ≠ loss. (c) Under (b)'s hypotheses with V(S, d) = loss: V(S ∪ {2}, d) ≠ win.
**Evidence.** Refuted. catch-up-research@b0853b4: (a) `CatchUp/UnitGuardObstruction.lean`, `not_unit_guard_nonloss`: R = {2,4,7,13,15,19,35,36,50,57,60,99} loses at deficit 1, every pick losing. (b), (c) `CatchUp/NearDeficitGuardObstruction.lean`, `not_near_deficit_nonloss`, `not_insert_two_nonwin`: S = {4,7,13,19,36,37,50,57,60,99} loses at deficit 14 and S ∪ {2} wins (via 13); also `not_copy_winning_move`: a winning pick on (S, 1) can lose on (S ∪ {2}, 1). Recheck: `position_value 1 2 4 7 13 15 19 35 36 50 57 60 99` → L; `position_value 14 4 7 13 19 36 37 50 57 60 99` → L; `position_value 14 2 4 7 13 19 36 37 50 57 60 99` → W (2+:W, 13+:W).
Rules out: non-loss from a small number or a number just below the deficit; adding a small number can turn a loss into a win.
