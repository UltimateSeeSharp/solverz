# C67 No short cycles in the shifted reply graph

**Statement.** For S with every element ≥ 3, 1 + sum(S) even and V(S, 1) = loss, the shifted reply graph (a → b: after the mover's pick a from (S, 1), the opponent's pick b wins) has no 2-cycle a ↔ b with [a, b] ⊆ S.
**Evidence.** Refuted. catch-up-research@b0853b4, `CatchUp/ShiftedReplyCycle.lean` (CU-L029), `no_loss_conditioned_local_span_exclusion`: S = {3,4,9,16,17,18,26,28,31,37} loses at deficit 1 with 3 → 4 → 3 (`obstruction_root_loss`, `obstruction_two_cycle`). Sliding a 2-cycle inward fails too (`no_full_span_minimum_slide`): {3,4,5,6,9,19,24,26,29,38} has 3 ↔ 5, but 5 → 4 draws and 4 → 5 loses. Positive part (CU-L030): a loss at ({3..N}, 1), N ≥ 7, forces a closed walk (`interval_loss_forces_closed_walk`). Recheck: `position_value 1 3 4 9 16 17 18 26 28 31 37` → L; `position_value 2 4 9 16 17 18 26 28 31 37` → 4:W; `position_value 3 3 9 16 17 18 26 28 31 37` → 3:W; `position_value 4 3 4 6 9 19 24 26 29 38` → 4:D; `position_value 3 3 5 6 9 19 24 26 29 38` → 5:L.
Rules out: proving ({3..N}, 1) ≠ loss by excluding cycles over local spans; an exclusion must use the whole interval.
