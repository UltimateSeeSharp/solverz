# C53 Symmetric sets draw

**Statement.** Every even-sum set S with S = (min S + max S) − S is a draw at a tie.
**Evidence.** Refuted. The smallest counterexamples (catch-up-research, CU-L019): {3, 4, 5} (= 8 − S) and {4, 5, 6, 7, 8} (= 12 − S) are tied player-1 wins, only by opening their minimum (`set_value 3 4 5`, `set_value 4 5 6 7 8` → W, rechecked 2026-10-30); 390 of the 1,954 even-total subsets of {1..12} with 2·max ≤ total are not draws. Also S = {1,6,8,9,14,15,17,22} (= 23 − S), a win only by opening 1 (journal 2026-10-26: review ideator, a halving induction; `set_value 1 6 8 9 14 15 17 22` → W). Rules out: arguments that use only the reflection x ↦ min + max − x.
