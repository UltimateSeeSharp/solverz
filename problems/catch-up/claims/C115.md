# C115 Inert opening depth

On {1..N} with an even total, every sequence of the first k(N) single picks (either player, any numbers, turns handed over by the real rule) reaches a draw; k(N) grows with N.

**Use.** I28 (not a route): only a measure of early slack. C1(N) is equivalent to every depth-k(N) position drawing, and those need the leader's defense like the root.
**Evidence.** Ideator fresh06 (`scratch/ideator/fresh06/inert.rs`, own margin table, matches A278587 for N ≤ 20): k(16) = 2, k(19) = k(20) = 3, k(23) = 4 (at pick 5, 13 of 201,867 positions are not draws). The first non-draws are mover wins by 2 at a deficit near N after an overshoot of about N (N = 23: {1..10, 13..16, 19..22} trailing by 21).
