# C78 Increasing turns

**Statement.** For a position with positive numbers and either player p: p can avoid loss iff it can while each run of its own consecutive picks is strictly increasing, against every opponent pick. After a least-safe pick (the smallest pick after which the mover can still avoid a loss; their `SafePick`/`LeastSafePick`) that keeps the turn, every safe next pick is larger.
**Use.** A defender's strategy (search, certificate) may take its picks within a turn in increasing order. At ({3..N}, 1) the two restricted checks decide the draw (`first_pair_draw_iff`); the `.p2` check gives C31(b)'s second hypothesis; both checks give the first-pair draw in `value_Icc_eq_draw_of_lower_pair_deletion_nonloss`.
**Proof.** Verified in our build, ported from catch-up-research@b0853b4 (2026-11-04; `lake build --wfail`, standard axioms): `Increasing.check_iff`, `Increasing.safe_next_gt`, `Increasing.LeastSafeTurn.pairwise`, `Increasing.first_pair_draw_iff` (`CatchUp/Increasing.lean`).
Also verified: a least-safe turn from any position reachable from an opening of I_N, with k picks before its catching pick, has k(k + 1) < 2N (`Increasing.LeastSafeTurn.reachable_preparation_length_bound`): those picks increase and sum to less than the deficit ≤ N.
