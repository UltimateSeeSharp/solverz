# C100 Dense intervals give C1

**Statement.** For every N ≥ 4 with N(N + 1)/2 even, H_N (C99 at N) implies V(I_N) = draw. Hence C99 for every even-total N ≥ 13 gives C1 (N ≤ 12: `CatchUp.conjecture_le_twelve`).

**Use.** Their August main route (catch-up-research, CU-L003; audit item X1), there as `value_Icc_eq_draw_of_dense_three_hole_nonloss` (archived `DenseHoleReduction.lean`, standard axioms there, not in our build). It uses only H_N's sets with hole 1: every Q_N(b) (holes {1, b}) and every child of Q_N(2) (holes {1, 2, x}); no H_N failure at N = 4..8 is a set C100 uses, so its condition holds at N = 4, 7, 8.
**Proof.** Sketch (paper). T = N(N + 1)/2 even, N ≥ 4. C31 (b)'s proof is per N (via C29), so it suffices that V({2..N}, 1) ≠ win and V({3..N}, 1) ≠ win. (i) Every pick b ≥ 2 of ({2..N}, 1) catches up and leaves Q_N(b) = (I_N ∖ {1, b}, b − 1): 2 holes, d ≤ N − 1, sum T − 1 − b ≥ b − 1, effective total T − 2 even; H_N gives Q_N(b) ≠ loss, so no pick wins. (ii) Every pick x ≥ 3 of ({3..N}, 1) leaves (I_N ∖ {1, 2, x}, x − 1): 3 holes, d ≤ N − 1, sum T − 3 − x ≥ x − 1 (T ≥ 2N + 2 for N ≥ 4), effective total T − 4 even; H_N gives ≠ loss. So V(I_N) = draw. H_N also gives ({3..N}, 1) ≠ loss directly, so C82 at N. Lean (later): as `conjecture_of_lower_ray`, N ≤ 12 by `conjecture_le_twelve`, N ≥ 13 by `value_Icc_eq_draw_of_lower_pair_deletion_nonloss` (C31 Evidence). Open until the refuter and Lean.
**Evidence.** Their route to C99 for every N, historical (archived Lean, standard axioms there, not in our build; data not replayed):
- Step: for N ≥ 4 with N + 1 even and H_N, H_{N+1} ⇔ adding the top N + 1 keeps every H_N non-loss a non-loss (`dense_three_hole_nonloss_succ_iff_add_top_preserves`, `DenseHoleSuccessor`); the top-absent, d = max and uncatchable cases are discharged there.
- Step-up condition: SmallGapFourHoleNonwin(M) := every (S, d) with S ⊆ I_M, ≤ 4 holes, d ≤ 4, d + sum(S) even (catchability not required) has V ≠ win. For N ≥ 4, N + 1 even: H_N and SmallGapFourHoleNonwin(N + 1) ⇒ H_{N+1} (`dense_three_hole_nonloss_succ_of_small_gap_four_hole_nonwin`, `DenseAddTopWindow`): after adding the top, some catching pick x ∈ [d, d + 4] leaves a non-win (`DenseAddTopWindowSafe N`), its child having ≤ 4 holes and d ≤ 4. Data: the window has 0 failures in 69,510 roots at odd N = 11..23, width 4 sharp (N = 11, 15, 19, 23, e.g. holes {1, 2, 3}, d = 0); SmallGapFourHoleNonwin fails (mover wins exist) at M = 12, 14 and holds at M = 16, 18, 20, 22, 24.
- Further reductions (`SmallGapTurnEndpoint`, `SmallGapShiftedTailPackage`): three completed-turn endpoint strata (no loss at even M = 16..24; last loss at M = 15, ({8..15}, 6), rechecked → L), then a shifted-tail package (CU-L014: fails at M = 16..19, holds at M = 20..28, 61,746,189 roots at M = 25..28); proved at no M.
**Tried.** The step covers only even tops N + 1; odd tops have no bridge, so the induction stops at the first odd N, and there is no two- or four-step form (their final report). Universal add-top preservation fails: `AddTopPreservesDenseThreeHoleNonloss 5` is false (their report, witness not kept); ours (`position_value`): ({2,4}, 2) = D (3 holes in I_5), ({2,4,6}, 2) = L; likewise ({1,5}, 4) and ({2,5}, 5) win, and lose once 6 is added. An every-descendant or every-common-turn induction (their add-top duality) fails at empty and singleton descendants. So the safe catch is chosen per position (the window), not by a uniform rule.
