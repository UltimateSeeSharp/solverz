# C24 Blocks at the doubling-free height

For p ≥ 1 and a ≥ 2p − 1: V({a..a+2p−1}, p mod 2) = draw. Route: Hyb2 (Z answers with the smallest number leaving Y's debt ≥ c of what is left, else AM+T's answer: the largest, or on an exact tie the second largest; as first picker the largest). (a) Debt bound: along every rounds-game play against Hyb2 on {0..2p−1} from debt p mod 2 (both roles), every round start has debt ≤ 2p − 1 + min R (R the offsets left; C25)

**Proof (given (a)).** (b) *Transfer.* Let KZ, KY be the round starts (Z, resp. Y, to move) reached by plays consistent with Hyb2 from either start; they are closed under Hyb2's rounds by definition, since Hyb2 depends only on the current state. C22 in its formalized form (`valueAux_transfer`) needs at each of them only offsets ≤ a and debt d ≤ a + o for every offset o left (any single pick of the mover catches up), plus `hendZ`: a play ending at a Z start ends at debt 0, which is (c) + (d). With (a) and a ≥ 2p − 1 the bounds hold at every round start against Hyb2. (c) *Guarantee.* Before Hyb2 first finds a valid answer, Z plays AM+T (as first picker the largest), so play stays in C23's depth class (KZ, KY of C23): Z's margin ≥ −1. At the first switch Y's new debt is ≥ c(R''), Y keeps the move, and from a Y round start with debt e ≥ c(R) the C20 partner is a valid answer (C20's partner step c(R − {i, π(i)}) = c(R) − |π(i) − i|, `exists_partner` in Lean, not C20's strict statement), so Hyb2 always answers validly and Y's debt stays ≥ c of what is left; at the end Y's margin is ≤ 0. (d) Parity as in C21. (a) is C25: Y's round-start debts ≤ 2p − 1 + min R, Z's ≤ 2p − 1 ≤ a, which is all (b) needs.
**Evidence.** `hyb2_states 9 hyb2 reach`: (a) holds for p ≤ 9 with equality for p ≥ 3 (the bound is reached with {0, 1} left, Y trailing by 2p − 1, after Y takes i and Z i + 1 in each round after the switch, e.g. p = 4: Z takes 7, Y 2; Y takes 3, Z 4; Y takes 5, Z 6). Z's round-start debts are ≤ 1 + min R. `hyb2_states 8`: in the real game at every a ∈ [2p − 1, 4p], p ≤ 8, no Y round start reached against Hyb2 allows a two-pick turn and Z secures ≥ 0 in both roles. `hyb2_states 7 hyb2 thresholds`: Hyb2 draws from a = 2p − 1 exactly (p ≥ 3; 1, 2 for p = 1, 2), as the ideator found; below it the last pair goes to Y in one turn. The step-wise closure of "c(R) ≤ e ≤ 2p − 1 + min R" fails over arbitrary R (`scratch/b20/hyb2_closure.py`, e.g. R = {0,1,2,3}, e = 3, Y takes 2: the only valid answer is 3, e' = 4), so a proof needs the structure of the reachable states.
