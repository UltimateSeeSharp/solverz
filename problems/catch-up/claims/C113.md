# C113 Small change under a block (POOL)

For p ≥ 1, B = {a..a+2p−1} and a set P of distinct positive numbers with max P ≤ p + 1 and a ≥ max(2p − 1, p + sum(P) + 3): every d ≤ p + sum(P) with d + sum(P ∪ B) even has V(P ∪ B, d) = draw; and the row is m(P ∪ B, d) = −max(0, d − p − sum(P)) for draw-parity d < a, except near d = a − 1 and at the lowest heights with |P| ≤ 1.

**Use.** I26 (dead as a route). An exact law for a two-scale family whose optimal turns take several numbers (pool numbers, then one block number); P = ∅ is C24's row (C86). A Lean target extending `block_row_draw_hyb2` with a pool term in C25's slack.
**Evidence.** Ideator fresh05 (`scratch/ideator/fresh05/pool7.rs`, own engine): 542 cases (P ⊆ {1..9}, p ≤ 7, a + 2p − 1 ≤ 22), no failure with max P ≤ p + 1; P = {p + 2} alone fails at d = 0 (+2). Complete and incomplete pools alike; pair sets in place of the block give isolated +2 wins.
**Tried.** A defender barred from buying pool numbers while it trails by more than p + sum(P) loses 53 of 331 positions: the answerer must sometimes buy the last small change.
**Proof.** Open.
