# C118 Extra-number bound

For every set S, number x ∉ S and deficit e: |m(S ∪ {x}, e) − m(S, e)| ≤ x (an added number moves the mover's margin by at most its size).

**Use.** A potential tool (ideator fresh07), with C117; at the root it was meant to sandwich C1 between {2..N} and {1, 1, 2..N}.
**Evidence.** The lower half (adding x costs the mover at most x) holds inside {1..17} (ideator `add.rs`).
**Tried.** Refuted (2026-11-05, [E43](../experiments/E43.md), two engines): upper half at {4, 7, 9, 10, 12, 15}: adding 1 moves e = 0 from −1 to 2 and e = 1 from −2 to 1; adding 2 moves e = 1 from −2 to 2. Lower half at {2, 5, 9, 11, 12, 14, 18}: adding 1 at e = 1 moves 2 to −1. Opposite recorded: one added number can move the margin by more than its size, both ways. The root sandwich was false anyway: {2..N} at a tie is −1 at N = 8, 11 and {1, 1, 2..N} is +1 at most N (ideator `nb.rs`).
**Proof.** Refuted.
