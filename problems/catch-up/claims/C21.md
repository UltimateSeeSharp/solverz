# C21 High pair sets at linear height

E a pair set with p ≥ 1 pairs, offsets ≥ 0, m = max E. If a > 3m: V({a + o : o ∈ E}, p mod 2) = draw. For blocks {a..a+2p−1}: a ≥ 6p − 2 (C16 needs a > 1 + p(2p−1))

**Strategy (Hyb, the ideator's, 2026-10-26).** Z takes the largest number left, as first picker and as answerer, until the first round start where Y moves at a debt e > c(R) (R the numbers left, c as in C20); from then on Z answers each Y pick with its C20 partner. In the real game, rounds are numbered as in C22.
**Proof.** Work in the rounds game R(E, g) with g = p mod 2 ≤ 1; p ≥ 1 gives m ≥ 1 ≥ g. Every R ⊆ E has c(R) ≤ max R − min R ≤ m (the matched gaps are disjoint intervals).
- *(a) Debts.* Along every play consistent with Hyb, every round start has debt ≤ 3m. Before the switch Z picks the largest. Z moving at debt d: Z takes t1, Y some t_j; δ = d + t_j − t1 ≤ d, so Z moves next at ≤ d, or Y at t1 − t_j − d ≤ m. Y moving at e ≤ c(R) ≤ m (no switch): Y takes i, Z the largest j left; Y moves next at e + j − i ≤ 2m, or Z at i − j − e ≤ m (only if i > j, so i = t1, j = t2). So before the switch Z's debts are ≤ max(g, m) and Y's ≤ max(g, 2m). After the switch only Y moves (C20), and each round raises its debt by at most the removed pair's gap; these gaps sum to c(R) ≤ m at the switch. So every debt is ≤ max(g, 2m) + m = 3m.
- *(b) Same game.* By (a) and a > 3m ≥ max E, C22 applies to σ = Hyb: every real-game play against Hyb is a rounds-game play against Hyb with the same outcome.
- *(c) Guarantee.* A play that never switches is a whole always-max play from an C15 start (Z moving at debt g ≤ 1, or Z as the other leading by g ≥ 0), so C15 gives Z's final margin ≥ −1; C15 holds for every play, so it applies play by play. A play that switches is a loss for Y by C20 alone.
- *(d) Parity.* The rounds game takes all numbers, so the final margin is g plus a difference of the two players' totals, ≡ g + sum(E) ≡ g + p ≡ 0 (mod 2), and ≥ −1 means ≥ 0. Both players secure at least a draw this way, so R(E, g) under Hyb gives Z ≥ draw in both roles, and by (b) so does V: the value is a draw. ∎
