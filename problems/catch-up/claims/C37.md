# C37 Gap bound (LG)

**Statement.** S a finite multiset of positive integers, d ≥ 0, m the margin game (C33). γ(W) = the largest gap between consecutive elements of sorted W ∪ {0} (repeated elements give 0; γ({1..N}) = 1; γ(∅) = 0). LG: m(S, d) ≥ −γ(S ⊎ {d}): the player to move never loses by more than the largest gap of the numbers left, its deficit counted as one more number. G (tie form): |m(S, 0)| ≤ γ(S).
**Reductions (paper, refuter pending).** (1) LG ⇒ G: the lower half is LG at d = 0; for the upper half, every opening x hands the opponent (S − x, x) with (S − x) ⊎ {x} = S, so A(S, x) ≥ −γ(S) and no opening is worth more than γ(S). (2) G ⇒ LG: for d = 0, LG is G's lower half (γ(S ⊎ {0}) = γ(S)); for d ≥ 1, at a tie on T = S ⊎ {d}, opening d is worth −m(S, d) ≤ m(T, 0) ≤ γ(T); this uses G on multisets (T has a repeat when d ∈ S). (3) G({1..N}) ⇒ C1(N): |m| ≤ 1; every play takes all numbers, so the final margin has the parity of the total (m(S, g) ≡ sum(S) + g mod 2), hence m = 0 for an even total, and V = sign(m) = draw (C33). Refuter, proof mode (2026-10-28): (1)–(3) SURVIVE; fixes applied (γ(∅) = 0, the clause d = 0).
**Why it is different.** It is a statement about every position, so an induction has its hypothesis everywhere and needs no family closed under play (C43, C45, C49, C56), and nothing local about one position (C42, C46, C58). Catch-Up as differencing: a pick x replaces {d, x} in V = S ⊎ {d} by |d − x|, the new deficit; always-max self-play is greedy number partitioning, whose discrepancy is at most γ(V) (ideator, own derivation); LG says the trailer keeps that bound against an adversary. Obstruction sets satisfy it with big gaps (C48's set: γ = 75; {2,2,2,3,3} wins by exactly γ = 2).
**Open part.** A proof: the two-ply induction "a move after which every reply keeps γ ≤ γ(root)" fails at intervals (taking a middle number makes γ = 2), so it needs an amortized potential, as C15 and C25 did.
