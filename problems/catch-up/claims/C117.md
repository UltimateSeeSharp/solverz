# C117 Deficit Lipschitz

For every set S and deficit d: m(S, d + 1) ≥ m(S, d) − 1 (C33's margin; m + d is non-decreasing in d).

**Use.** A potential tool (ideator fresh07): with C118 it would give a Lipschitz calculus for margins, and at C1's root it would pass sign information between neighbouring deficits.
**Evidence.** Holds on every S ⊆ {1..17} at every d (ideator `lip2.rs`, 4.6M positions at 16) and on multisets over {1..7} with multiplicity ≤ 3; E43's check: every subset of {1..10}.
**Tried.** Refuted (2026-11-05, [E43](../experiments/E43.md), two engines): ({1, 5, 9, 11, 12, 14, 18}, d = 1) has m = 1, at d = 2 m = −2; ({1, 2, 5, 9, 11, 12, 14, 18}, 0 → 1): 2 → −1; ({4, 5, 6, 8, 9, 16, 18}, 4 → 5): 2 → −1. Opposite recorded: the margin can fall by 3 when the deficit grows by 1, first at sets with maximum 18 (3 of 22.9M positions inside {1..18}), as C60 broke late. Its copying proof needs m(T, 0) + m(T, 1) ≥ −1, false at T = {4, 7, 9, 10, 12, 15} (−1 and −2).
**Proof.** Refuted.
