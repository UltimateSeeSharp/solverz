# C74 Interior and boundary of the lower ray

**Statement.** N ≥ 7, R_b = {2..N} ∖ {b−1, b}. If ({3..N}, 1) draws and for every 3 ≤ b ≤ N: (interior) V(R_b ∖ {y−1, y}, 1) ≠ loss whenever y−1, y, y+1 ∈ R_b; (boundary) for y ∈ R_b ∩ {2, b−2, b+1, N}, V(R_b ∖ {y}, y) ≠ loss (opening y of the tied R_b does not win); then V(I_N) = draw.
**Use.** Refines C31 (b): Q_N(b) ≠ loss follows once the tied R_b is no win for its mover (the exact pick b−1 reaches it), and a winning opening y of R_b with both neighbors present forces the interior loss (the neighbor jump of C64, C65).
**Proof.** Verified in our build, ported from catch-up-research@b0853b4 (2026-11-04; `lake build --wfail`, standard axioms): `value_Icc_eq_draw_of_lower_ray_interior_and_boundary` (`CatchUp/LowerRayInteriorReduction.lean`).
**Evidence.** Mine (2026-10-30, `scratch/a14/ray.rs`, one table over {1..24}): the hypotheses hold for every even-total N from 11 to 24 (({3..N}, 1) draws; interior 42 to 380 cases, boundary 32 to 84, none losing); they fail only at N = 4, 7, 8, on boundary cases. The interior family, {2..N} minus j disjoint adjacent pairs at deficit 1 (j = 2 here), has no loss for j ≤ 6 and N ≤ 24 (`pairdel.rs`, up to 12,376 sets per N); the first losses come at j = 8, C65's {6, 11, 14, 15, 18, 23} at N = 23 and {6, 13, 14, 17, 20, 21, 24} at N = 24 (`position_value 1 6 13 14 17 20 21 24` → L). In the interior family (j = 2) the mover's smallest number is always a non-losing pick from N = 16 to 24 (`twodel.rs`; not at N = 11, 12, 15, e.g. a = 4, c = 6 at N = 15), so there the interior case reduces to ({3..N} minus two adjacent pairs, 1) being no win for its mover, C31 (b)'s second start with two pairs gone.
