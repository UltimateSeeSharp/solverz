# C74 Interior and boundary of the lower ray

**Statement.** N ≥ 7, R_b = {2..N} ∖ {b−1, b}. If ({3..N}, 1) draws and for every 3 ≤ b ≤ N: (interior) V(R_b ∖ {y−1, y}, 1) ≠ loss whenever y−1, y, y+1 ∈ R_b; (boundary) for y ∈ R_b ∩ {2, b−2, b+1, N}, V(R_b ∖ {y}, y) ≠ loss (opening y of the tied R_b does not win); then V(I_N) = draw.
**Use.** Refines C31 (b): Q_N(b) ≠ loss follows once the tied R_b is no win for its mover (the exact pick b−1 reaches it), and a winning opening y of R_b with both neighbors present forces the interior loss (the neighbor jump of C64, C65).
