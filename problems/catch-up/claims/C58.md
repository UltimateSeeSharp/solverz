# C58 Near ties stay drawn across a breakpoint

**Evidence.** (log 2026-10-28: near ties on optimal lines flip across C35's breakpoints): on {1..N−4} ∪ ({N−3..N} + c), a pick on an optimal line just below an integer breakpoint c* that leaves the picker a hair ahead or behind (one quadruple number of imbalance) reaches a drawn node, but the same pick just above c* reaches a node the picker wins or loses: N = 11 at c* = 1 (12 nodes), N = 12 at four integer breakpoints, e.g. c* = 1, R = {2,4,6,7,q2,q3,q4}, picker a hair ahead: draw below, win above. Rules out: crossing a breakpoint by B53's lemma in its strong form (every near-tie node on optimal lines is a draw on both sides); the root keeps its value only because the defender can steer around such nodes.
