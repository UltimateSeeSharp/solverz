# C105 Tied odd blocks minus one number draw (B1)

For every k ≥ 2, height a ≥ 1, B = {a..a+2k} and h ∈ B with h − a ≡ k (mod 2) (equivalently sum(B) − h even): V(B ∖ {h}, 0) = draw.

**Use.** Root claim of I24, with C106. At a = 1, h = 2k + 1 it is C1 for N = 2k ≡ 0 mod 4 ({1..N} = {1..N+1} ∖ {N+1}). Height-free: unlike C86 and C85, it holds in the rounds regime too, where it is a finite one-pick game per k (C13): the tied mover on {0..2k} ∖ {o}.
**Evidence.** [E34](../experiments/E34.md) (committed, two engines): k ≤ 9 at every a ≤ 2(2k + 1) and a = (2k + 1)², no non-draw; |m| ≤ 1 at every hole of either parity. Ideator fresh04c (own solver `margin.py`): every odd block of size ≥ 5 inside {1..15}, every hole of the parity; sizes 5, 7, 9 at every a ≤ 24; sizes 11, 13 at a ≤ 10; size 9 at a = 40, size 11 at a = 60 (rounds regime); no non-draw. Mine (`scratch/a19/mg.rs`, margins): k ≤ 8 at every a ≤ 40, every hole of either parity: |m| ≤ 1, so the off-parity holes are ±1.
**Rounds half (scratch, `scratch/a19/rb1.rs`, `rb2.rs`, k ≤ 8).** In the rounds game on {0..2k} ∖ {o} at a tie, C23's AM+T draws every hole of the parity in both roles (Z first and Z answering), except o = 1 with k odd when the opponent moves first (AM+T −2; optimal play draws). There the line ends in the endgame {0, 2, 3, 4} with Z behind by 1, where the maximum loses and 3 or 2 draw. AM+T enters C23's class K after k − 1 − o rounds (Z first) or k − o (opponent first) against every reply, at once for o ≥ k − 1, i.e. o ≥ k of the parity (K needs s + q ≥ D_Z, i.e. o ≥ k − 1, at the start), never for o = 1, k odd. With exact play once 4 numbers are left, AM+T draws every hole of the parity in both roles (k ≤ 7). For o ≥ k − 1 the rounds half is C108 (paper proof from C23); below, a proof needs a pre-K phase and the endgame repair. Either gives C105 above the rounds threshold only.
**Tried.** No fixed answer rule (h − 1, h + 1, min, max, a + b − h) draws every case (ideator run 6); C23's depth class needs s ≥ 1 with a hole, so even the rounds half needs a new class. After the tied mover's pick x the position ({B ∖ {h, x}}, x) can be far from a draw (margins up to +7 for k ≤ 6, `mg.rs b2h`), so |m| ≤ 1 does not pass to the two-hole positions.
**Proof.** Open.
