# C109 Tied odd blocks minus one number draw at every real height

For every k ≥ 2, every o ∈ {0..2k} with o ≡ k (mod 2) and every real h > 0: the tied mover on {h + x : x ∈ {0..2k} ∖ {o}} draws. Equivalently: an arithmetic progression of 2k + 1 terms, first term a > 0 and difference δ > 0, without its term of index o, is a draw at a tie, whatever a/δ.

**Use.** Strengthens C105 (the integer heights) to every real height, I24. Contains C32's statement for p = k even (o = 0 or 2k: a block of 2k numbers at a tie) and C1 for N ≡ 0 mod 4 (h = 1, o = 2k). Unlike the band, whose interior entries flip between integer heights (C88), this family has no breakpoint at all on (0, ∞): a continuity argument from the rounds regime (h large, C108) down to h = 1 would not have to cross a change of value, only changes of the game tree (C32's near-tie mechanism).
**Evidence.** [E37](../experiments/E37.md) (committed exact parametric engine `Param`): k ≤ 7, every draw-parity hole, a draw at every h > 0. Scratch (`scratch/a19/mg.rs realh`, integer engine at h = r/q, q = 3..8): no non-draw, and every hole of the other parity stays within one unit of margin.
**Tried.** Monotonicity in h as the mechanism (scratch `uni/mono.rs`, 2026-11-05): if tied values were monotone in h, C109 and C1 would follow from the two ends (rounds regime, h → 0+). False: of the 511 even-size offset sets inside {0..9}, 63 are not monotone on open intervals (e.g. {0, 2, 3, 5, 6, 7}: W on (1/2, 4), L on (4, 6), W after 6). One strategy for all heights (`scratch/a19/uni`, `Param`, k ≤ 5): the tied mover has an opening that draws at every h only at k = 2 and (k, o) = (3, 1); from k = 3 on it must choose by height. The answerer has a first answer good at every h to every opening for k = 2, 4, 5, but not at k = 3 (o = 3 after the opening 4, o = 5 after 0). So the invariance is in the value, not in one strategy (as for C32, whose pieces grow); a continuity argument has to cross breakpoints where the strategy changes. At k = 3, o = 3 the tied mover needs two pieces that overlap: opening 1 draws exactly on (0, 6), opening 4 on [1/2, 1] ∪ [4, ∞) (the maximum 6 from 6): small openings for the low phase, large ones for the rounds phase (`uni/src/bin/show.rs`).
**Proof.** Open.
