# C109 Tied odd blocks minus one number draw at every real height

For every k ≥ 2, every o ∈ {0..2k} with o ≡ k (mod 2) and every real h > 0: the tied mover on {h + x : x ∈ {0..2k} ∖ {o}} draws. Equivalently: an arithmetic progression of 2k + 1 terms, first term a > 0 and difference δ > 0, without its term of index o, is a draw at a tie, whatever a/δ.

**Use.** Strengthens C105 (the integer heights) to every real height, I24. Contains C32's statement for p = k even (o = 0 or 2k: a block of 2k numbers at a tie) and C1 for N ≡ 0 mod 4 (h = 1, o = 2k). Unlike the band, whose interior entries flip between integer heights (C88), this family has no breakpoint at all on (0, ∞): a continuity argument from the rounds regime (h large, C108) down to h = 1 would not have to cross a change of value, only changes of the game tree (C32's near-tie mechanism).
**Evidence.** [E37](../experiments/E37.md) (committed exact parametric engine `Param`). Scratch (`scratch/a19/mg.rs realh`, integer engine at h = r/q, q = 3..8): no non-draw, and every hole of the other parity stays within one unit of margin.
