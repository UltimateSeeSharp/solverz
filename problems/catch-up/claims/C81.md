# C81 Dense pair deletions never lose

**Statement.** For N with N(N + 1)/2 even and S = {2..N} minus j disjoint pairs {y − 1, y}, j even and 4j ≤ N: V(S, 1) ≠ loss.
**Use.** j = 2 is C74's interior hypothesis (R_b minus a second pair); the general j is the family that hypothesis falls into when answered pair by pair.
**Evidence.** Mine (2026-10-30, `scratch/a14/pairdel.rs`, one table over {1..24}): no loss for j ≤ 6 at every even-total N ≤ 24 (up to 12,376 sets per N), beyond the bound 4j ≤ N; the first losses come at j = 8 with 6 or 7 numbers left (C65's {6, 11, 14, 15, 18, 23} at N = 23; {6, 13, 14, 17, 20, 21, 24} at N = 24, `position_value` → L). At j = 2 the smallest number is a non-losing pick from N = 16 (C74).
