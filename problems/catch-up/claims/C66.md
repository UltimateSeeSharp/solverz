# C66 Geometry-free first-pair transfer

**Statement.** For finite S with 3 ∈ S, every element ≥ 3 and 1 + sum(S) even: if V(S ∖ {3}, 2) = win (the pick 3 from (S, 1) loses), then V(S, 1) ≠ loss.
**Evidence.** Refuted. catch-up-research@b0853b4, `CatchUp/FirstPairDescentObstruction.lean` (CU-L028), `no_geometry_free_three_reserve_transfer`: S = {3,7,9,14,17,20,29,34} has V(S, 1) = loss (`sparse_loss`) and V(S ∖ {3}, 2) = win (`sparse_shadow_win`); every first pick from (S, 1) leaves the opponent a win (`sparse_all_first_children_win`). Recheck: `position_value 1 3 7 9 14 17 20 29 34` → L (every pick L); `position_value 2 7 9 14 17 20 29 34` → W (14:W).
Rules out: a strategy steal from ({3..N}, 1) to its shadow ({4..N}, 2) that keeps only min = 3 and parity and drops the interval's geometry.
