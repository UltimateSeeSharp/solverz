# C86 The draw band at every height (BAND)

For all p ≥ 1, a ≥ 1 and 0 ≤ d ≤ p with d ≡ p (mod 2), except (p, a, d) = (2, 2, 2): V({a..a+2p−1}, d) = draw.

**Use.** Root claim of I17. Implies C12 (d = p mod 2) and, through C27's families (Lean `conjecture_of_two_families`: {1..N} at 0 for N ≡ 0 mod 4, {2..N} at 1 for N ≡ 3 mod 4), C1. Lean has it for a ≥ 2p − 1 (`block_row_draw_hyb2`, C24). Relation to C37: at a = 1, {1..2p} ⊎ {d} is complete (γ = 1), so LG gives m ≥ −1, and with the parity (m ≡ d + p ≡ 0) the non-loss half for every d ≤ 2p; C86 adds the half "the mover does not win" for d ≤ p (above p it can win, C86's run 4).
**Link to C84.** At a = 1, ({1..2p}, d) is the position after opening the duplicate d of the tied multiset {1..2p} ⊎ {d}, which is C84's member with L = 2p + 1 and ε_j = 1 exactly for j > d (parity deficit 0). So C86 at a = 1 makes that opening a draw, and the level law (C28) gives C84 for these members.
**Evidence.** Ideator (2026-11-04, `scratch/ideator/fresh04/rows.rs`, run 3, unchecked): every p ≤ 7, a ≤ 2p + 1; the only non-draw in the band is {2, 3, 4, 5} at deficit 2 (C47). At real heights the lowest entry d = p mod 2 is a draw for every h ≥ 1/2 (C32, p ≤ 8). The rest of the row is not height-invariant (run 4: 280 of 679 entries differ at low heights, all in the trailer's favour).
**Tried.** By hand, how the diagonal survives at a = 1 (2026-11-04): ({1..6}, 3) draws because the trailer's two-pick turn {1, 2} makes exact change to a tie on {3, 4, 5, 6} (C11), and after the opening 6 the reply 3 is exact change to a tie on {1, 2, 4, 5} (C11). So below 2p − 1 the band rests on exact-change turns that remove whole matched pairs (C77's blocks landing on pair-set draws), not on one-pick rounds.
**Proof.** Open. p ≤ 4 in Lean (2026-11-04, written, not yet compiled; Q6): `CatchUp.band_draw_le_four` and the exception `band_exception` (`CatchUp/Band.lean`): `block_row_draw_hyb2` from a ≥ 2p − 1, the verified evaluator below.
