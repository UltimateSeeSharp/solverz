# C79 Explicit certificates grow exponentially

**Statement.** For N ≥ 16, 12t + 2 ≤ N and either player: every valid full strategy DAG certifying that player's non-loss from I_N in their `InitialCertificate` format (atomic picks, every opponent edge, safe terminal leaves) has at least 2^t nodes.
**Use.** Only for that certificate format, not for Lean proofs, symbolic strategies or compressed certificates: a finite certificate per N cannot prove C1 for all N; a proof needs a symbolic strategy or invariant (our inference).
**Proof.** Verified in our build, ported from catch-up-research@b0853b4 (2026-11-04; `lake build --wfail`, standard axioms): `initial_certificate_exponential` (`CatchUp/Earlier/CertificateComplexityHighCounters.lean`).
