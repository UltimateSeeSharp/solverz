# C75 Every opening draws iff every pair is neutral

**Statement.** For N ≥ 3: every opening of I_N draws (V(I_N ∖ {a}, a) = draw for every a) iff every ordered pair a ≠ b of I_N is neutral: after opening a, player 2's pick b is worth a draw to it.
**Use.** Reformulates C2 as a statement about two-pick continuations.
