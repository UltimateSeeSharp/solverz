# C44 Forced partner reply

**Evidence.** (log 2026-10-22: answering with the partner is not a strategy): a defender that starts every turn with the adjacent partner of the attacker's last pick, and plays perfectly otherwise, loses from N = 11 (as player 1) and N = 12, 15, 16 (both sides) to an attacker that never blunders; e.g. N = 11: at R = {4,5,7,8,9}, g = 3, one broken pair, the forced 7 loses and only 4 draws. Rules out: a pairing strategy in which the partner reply is unconditional.
