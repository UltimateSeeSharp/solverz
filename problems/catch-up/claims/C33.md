# C33 Sign lemma

**Statement.** For every finite S and g ≥ 0: V(S, g) = sign(m(S, g)), where m(S, g) is the margin game's value for the mover trailing by g (same moves and turn hand-overs as Catch-Up; no early-loss rule: a mover who cannot catch up keeps taking numbers; payoff the mover's final score minus the opponent's; both players optimize it). m is defined on finite multisets (a pick removes one copy); the proof works there unchanged. V uses the non-opening flag; the opening flag only differs at g > 0, and value(S) = V(S, 0) (C13's section, `valueAux_true_eq_false`).
**Proof.** Same move sets and the same hand-overs. Terminal positions agree in sign: S = ∅ gives sign(−g) for both; a Catch-Up early loss (sum(S) < g) is a margin-game line where the mover takes everything and ends at sum(S) − g < 0, and every line from there ends there. sign is non-decreasing and odd, so it commutes with max and with negation; induction on |S|.
