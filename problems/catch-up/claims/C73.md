# C73 Lower-ray endpoints

**Statement.** Q_N(b) = (I_N ∖ {1, b}, b − 1). (a) N ≥ 3: Q_N(N) ≠ loss. (b) N ≥ 7: Q_N(N−1) ≠ loss. (c) N ≥ 7 and V(I_{N−4} ∖ {1}, 1) = draw (opening 1 of I_{N−4} draws): Q_N(N−2) ≠ loss. (d) N ≥ 15, or N ≥ 11 with N(N+1)/2 even: Q_N(N−3) ≠ loss. (e) N ≥ 23: Q_N(N−4) ≠ loss. No parity premise except in (d)'s second form.
**Use.** Five fixed-distance points of C31 (b)'s ray: V({2..N}, 1) ≠ win iff Q_N(b) ≠ loss for every 2 ≤ b ≤ N. The middle of the ray is open, and descent in the tail length is blocked (C71).
**Proof.** Verified in our build, ported from catch-up-research@b0853b4 (2026-11-04; `lake build --wfail`, standard axioms): (a) `pair_deletion_position_top_value_ne_loss` (`CatchUp/Earlier/DeficitWinningBound.lean`); (b) `pair_deletion_position_penultimate_value_ne_loss` (`CatchUp/LowerRayEndpoints.lean`); (c) `pair_deletion_position_antepenultimate_value_ne_loss_of_opening_one_draw` (`CatchUp/LowerRayAntepenultimateDescent.lean`); (d) `pair_deletion_position_fourth_from_top_value_ne_loss_of_fifteen_le`, `pair_deletion_position_fourth_from_top_value_ne_loss` (`CatchUp/LowerRayFourthEndpoint.lean`); (e) `pair_deletion_position_fifth_from_top_value_ne_loss` (`CatchUp/LowerRayFifthEndpoint.lean`). (c)–(e): a loss at Q_N(b) forces a winning opening above the exact pick on the tied residual F(m, k) (C77 (c)), each excluded by exact-sum blocks.
**Evidence.** `position_value` on Q_N(N−k), k ≤ 4, 5 ≤ N ≤ 20: no loss from N = 7 (draws at even total from N = 12; wins at Q_7(7), Q_7(6), Q_11(10); wins at odd totals (checked to N = 18)); Q_6(5) loses, so (b)'s bound is sharp.
