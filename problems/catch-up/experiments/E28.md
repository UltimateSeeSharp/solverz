# E28 How low can a block keep the rounds structure (I17's mechanism)

**Tests.** C88's mechanism and I17: whether some Z strategy keeps every round-start debt ≤ a + min R (so every single pick catches up and the real game at height a is the rounds game, C22) while drawing the band entry. **Decides.** Least height a*(p, d) well below 2p − 1 → a lower provable high base for the band (a Hyb2-like strategy with a smaller debt bound to find and prove); a*(p, d) ≈ 2p − 1 → C24's height is the limit of rounds-structure arguments, and the band below it needs multi-pick play (C86 below 2p − 1 must come from elsewhere).

**Outcome.** The least height at which some Z strategy keeps every round-start debt ≤ a + min R and draws: p = 1..8 off the diagonal 1, 2, 4, 5, 7, 8, 10, 12 (C24: 1, 3, 5, 7, 9, 11, 13, 15), the diagonal one lower at odd p; about ⌊(3p − 1)/2⌋. So a rounds-structure argument can reach down to about 3p/2, a third of the way below C24, but never to a = 1: below about 3p/2 the band needs multi-pick turns (C86 Tried).
