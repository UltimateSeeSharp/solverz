# E31 Simple Z rules under the one-pick cap (C86 below C24's height)

**Tests.** C86 between E28's least height and C24's 2p − 1: is there a simple rule (a Hyb2 variant) that keeps every round-start debt ≤ a + min R and draws each band entry? **Decides.** A rule reaching E28's optimum → a candidate theorem "C86 for a ≥ ≈ 3p/2" to prove; none → the capped strategy is not local, and the band below 2p − 1 needs a new kind of argument.
**Command.** `e31_capped_rules 6`; `e31_capped_rules 5 breaks` · **Commit.** 0d13606 · **Date.** 2026-11-04
**Validated.** Built in: Hyb2/Max succeeds at a = 2p − 1 for every band entry (C24, C25). Free/Free reproduces E28's least heights exactly.
**Machine, Runtime (runs over a minute).** 1 thread, seconds.
**Checked.** Free/Free agrees with E28 (separate engine).

**Outcome.** No simple rule goes below 2p − 1. Every rule built on Hyb2's answers needs exactly 2p − 1, even with free first picks (so the answers, not the first picks, are the bottleneck); the tie answer, Hyb2 with the tie answer only when its own answer breaks the cap at once, the undercut answer (largest j ≤ i − e) and Hyb2 switching to undercut at 4 numbers left all fail or need 2p − 1; the optimum (Free/Free) is E28's ≈ (3p − 1)/2. Where Hyb2's answer fails at the least height (1, 3, 35, 366 reached Y moves for p = 3, 4, 5 at d = p mod 2), the drawing answer is usually the largest j ≤ i − e (({0, 3, 5, 6}, Y at 2 takes 6: draws with 3, Hyb2 answers 5; ({0, 1, 6, 7}, Y at 0 takes 1: draws with 0, Hyb2 answers 7)), but as a standing rule it loses; the capped strategy depends on more than the position's local data.
