# I31 Rule variants that separate the lemmas from C1

Find a rule variant of Catch-Up that keeps the lemmas the routes use (parity law C3, sign lemma C33, the rounds game C13 with C18/C20/C24/C25 at high heights, the level law C28) but loses at an even total; it would name what the low phase lacks and show which arguments cannot work. Technique `T1011` prove the approach cannot work. Source: user's toolbox sweep, 2026-11-05.

**Kill test.** 3–4 rule variants, N ≤ 16, and which lemmas still hold in each. Run (scratch `scratch/a19/variant/var.rs`, margin game): a turn takes at least one number and continues while the mover's total is below the opponent's + θ and fewer than L numbers were taken (Catch-Up: θ = 0, L = ∞). Grid θ = −4..4, L ∈ {1, 2, 3, 4, ∞}, every even total N ≤ 15: every variant with L ≥ 2 draws at every even total except at small N (θ ≤ −2: up to N = 4 to 8; θ = 2: N = 3; θ = 3: up to N = 7 or 8; θ = 4: up to N = 12, e.g. −2 at N = 11); only L = 1 (strict alternation) loses at every N. Turns capped at 2 or 3 numbers, turns ending only when strictly ahead (θ = 1), or within 1 of catching up (θ = −1) all draw at every even total N ≤ 15.
Asymmetric rules (`asym.rs`, N ≤ 15): player 1 or player 2 alone capped at 2 numbers per turn (the turn passes even while behind), alone strict (θ = 1), or alone stopping within 1 (θ = −1): every one draws at every even total (one exception, player 2 within 1 at N = 4); player 2 limited to one number per turn loses at every N. So the draw at even totals is universal across catch-up-like turn rules: it needs only that each player may take a second number in a turn (to make change), and it does not depend on the tie rule (as C112), the stopping threshold or the turn length. Ordered turns (`order.rs`, N ≤ 15): every turn's numbers increasing, or every turn's numbers decreasing, both draw at every even total. In the simplest member, turns capped at 2 numbers, a partner defender still fails (`partner2.rs`: adjacent pairs (2i − 1, 2i) or (2i, 2i + 1), the attacker wins by up to 14 at N ≤ 16), so the low phase is not hard only because of the attacker's long turns. Sandwich with an attacker window (`window.rs`, N ≤ 15): the attacker may end its turn at any lead in [lo, hi] (must end at hi), the defender plays Catch-Up's rule. With [0, 1], [−1, 0] and [−1, 1] the defender still holds every even total in both roles (a defender proof there would give C1, as C111 does); with [0, 2] the attacker wins by 2 at every N (it may run on to a lead of 2). No variant in these families separates the routes' lemmas from C1: each has the analogous lemmas (its rounds game is C13's with the tie threshold shifted by θ) and draws.
Far variants (`far.txt`): L = 2 draws at N = 16, 19, 20; θ = 4 and θ = −4 draw at N = 16, 19, 20; θ = 5 draws at N = 16, 19, 20; θ = 6 loses at N = 16 and 20 (−2) and draws at N = 19.
High blocks at a tie (`var.rs blocks`, a = 20, 60, p = 2..6) have the same values under θ = 0, 6 and 8, so θ = 6 keeps the high phase and loses {1..16} and {1..20}: it separates what the high-phase arguments give from C1. The level law (C28: a tied position never has both a drawing and a winning opening) is not what it lacks: it fails already at θ = 2 (77 of 511 tied even-total sets inside {1..10} have both, `level.rs`; 167 at θ = 4, 165 at θ = 6), where C1 still holds; so the draw does not come from the level law either.
Edge (`edge.txt`): θ = 7 is a non-draw at every even total N = 7..20 (+2 at N = 19, 20), θ = 8 at every N = 8..20 tested (+2 at N = 19, 20; θ = 8 draws only at N = 7). So thresholds well above the small numbers break the draw far beyond small N, while high blocks keep Catch-Up's values; the draw rests neither on the high-phase arguments nor on the level law (which fails from θ = 2, where the draw still holds). It is not a lack of change either (`var.rs change`): under θ = 6, {1..16} stays at −2 with extra small numbers 1, 1 / 2, 2 / 1, 3 / 1, 1, 2, 2 added, and flips to +2 with 8, 8 or 15, 17 added (all draws under θ = 0); what θ ≥ 6 breaks is open.
**Status.** dead (question answered): within |θ| ≤ 5 and caps L ≥ 2 no variant separates; θ ≥ 6–7 separates the high phase from the draw, but what it breaks is not the level law and not a lack of small change; no new route.
