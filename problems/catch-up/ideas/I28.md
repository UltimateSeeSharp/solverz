# I28 Inert opening depth

The first few picks from {1..N} cannot spoil the draw (C115), so early play has slack; the only early mistakes are overshoots of about N. Not a reduction: the positions at that depth need the leader's defense as the root does. Kept as a fact for judging routes (one whose first steps need exact play from the root is over-constrained). Source: ideator, 2026-11-05 (`scratch/ideator/fresh06/`, A). Cut in the same round, with measurements there (`cuts.md`): one deficit threshold per set (false on sets reached by drawing play, e.g. {1..6, 9, 10, 11} draws trailing by 11, the mover wins by 2 trailing by 7); defender positions as unions of at most r intervals (r grows: 2 at N = 7..11, 3 at 12..16, 4 at 19..23); a fixed cap on the attacker's deficit at its turn starts (3 at N = 12, 16, 4 at N = 20).

**Kill test.** k(24) and k(27) (about 420 MB at N = 24; ask the user): k(27) ≤ 4 makes it a small-N effect.
**Status.** dead: not a route to C1 (C1 at N is equivalent to the depth-k(N) positions drawing); C115 stays as data.
