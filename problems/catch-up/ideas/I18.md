# I18 Non-loss below half the size

An invariant class for the defender in the low phase: C89 (NLB*), {k..N} minus any j ≤ |R|/4 adjacent pairs, |R| even, never loses at deficits d < min(k, |R|/2). The low phase's natural dynamics (O takes x, D answers x ± 1 or makes change from the bottom run) raise the base and keep the deficit below the minimum, so this family could be closed under D's play; it would then need a step lemma (some partner-or-change turn stays in the class without losing) and an exit (C24's blocks or a post-switch class), and gives C1 through C31 (b) and C81.
