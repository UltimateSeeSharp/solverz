# I17 The draw band at every height

Prove the whole draw band instead of one entry: C86 (BAND), blocks {a..a+2p−1} draw at every deficit d ≤ p of the parity p, at every height a ≥ 1 (one exception, (p, a, d) = (2, 2, 2)), and its pair-set form C87 (BAND-PD). C1 is BAND at (a, d) = (1, 0) for p even and (2, 1) for p odd (Lean `conjecture_of_two_families`; the reduction itself is `conjecture_of_band` in `CatchUp/Band.lean`, written 2026-11-04, not yet compiled); C12 is its entry d = p mod 2; C82 is (3, 1). Why the band: the high-height proofs (C23, C24's `block_row_draw_hyb2`) are band statements already, the single entry has no slack for an amortized argument (C72), and the near-tie flips across heights (C58) seem to hit only the diagonal d = p. Mechanism: a descent in a from C24's a ≥ 2p − 1 to a = 1 in which only the diagonal entry can be lost (C88, BANDSTEP). Outside the band nothing is height-invariant: 280 of 679 other row entries change at low heights, all toward the trailer (ideator run 4), so "rows at every height" is cut. Collapsed blocks: E26 measures the same band on C85's sets. Root claim: C86. Source: ideator, 2026-11-04 (`scratch/ideator/fresh04/`).

**Kill test.** Q-C1, `rows.exe 10 24` (queued): BAND for p ≤ 10 at every a ≤ 2p + 1 and BAND-PD for N ≤ 24. A non-draw off the diagonal kills C86/C87 as stated; a second diagonal exception is harmless. Then Q-C2 (BANDSTEP with the refuter's parametric solver, p = 4..6): only d = p changes across breakpoints → the off-diagonal band is height-invariant and only the diagonal needs its own argument; interior flips → the descent must split there.
