# I23 The low column of blocks

Prove C104 (EB): low blocks {a..a+n−1}, a ≤ n/2, draw at every deficit d ≤ n − 1 of the parity, for both parities of n. C1 is its entries (N, 1, 0) and (N − 1, 2, 1) through C27 (Lean `conjecture_of_two_families`). Why it could be easier than C86 below 2p − 1: the deficit is free (an opponent's single pick at a low block leaves the defender trailing by at most n − 1 + a − d, so an induction leaves the family only by holes, never by the deficit); it is two-sided with twice C86's entries at low heights, so a closed family is tested on more data; its no-loss half at a = 1 reduces to C106 by exact change (the trailer takes d, the tied opponent on {1..n} ∖ {d} never wins). Off the parity the entries are ±1: low blocks are parity-trivial. Source: ideator, 2026-11-04 (`scratch/ideator/fresh04c/`, A).
