# I15 Rank-perturbed intervals

Prove the exception-free family C84 (DI: {1..L} with any elements other than 1 lowered by one, at the parity deficit), which holds both twins and {1..L}; C1 is its member ε = 0. Reach the interval from twins by moving one element by 1 at a time, or by induction on L (top twins peel to a DI prefix inside DI, leaving members with a non-twin top, three top-pair types). Free one-sided transfer between neighbours (A ⊎ {x}, 0) and (A ⊎ {x + 1}, 1): the moved element's own option reaches the same (A, x), so a draw there gives the tie member a draw (C28) and the deficit-1 member ≥ draw. The exact neighbour coupling is not value-preserving (sign differs on 14–18% of pairs over A ⊆ {1..16}; e.g. ({1, 3}, 0): m = 2, ({2, 3}, 1): m = 0). At a tie every member (L ∈ {8, 10..13}) has a drawing opening among its three smallest values; opening 1 alone fails in 9 of 2,048 at L = 13. Root claim: C84.
