# I8 Low phase, high phase

- **Reduction.** The defender D plays in two phases. Low phase, while the set is low (min < span): D makes change with its k smallest numbers, turns of ≤ 2 picks, and steers into a class H of high positions (min ≥ span, debts and parity as in C25's class). High phase: from every position of H, D draws by Hyb2 (C25's potential W; C24 is the case of full blocks). C1 ⇐ (i) a high-phase lemma for H and (ii) a low-phase lemma: from {1..N} at gap 0, in both roles, D can enter H without losing. By C29 (Lean) the start can be {2..N} at deficit 1 (both roles); by C31 (refuter running) only the leader's defense is needed, from ({2..N}, 1) and ({3..N}, 1). Root claims: C24, C25 (high phase); C29, C31 (start).
- **Metric.** Largest N for which (ii) holds with a fixed k and H described explicitly, checked by search; then the nodes of (i) and (ii) at `paper proof`. Now: free low play then Hyb2 from the first high position draws {1..N} to N = 24 ([E8](../experiments/E8.md)); bottom-4 picks while low (free when high) draw to 24 ([E9](../experiments/E9.md)); H explicit and forceable from C31's starts and {1..N}: to N = 16 by formal pieces alone (C25's debt bound, C23's class, e ≥ c), to N = 24 with Hyb2T's exact guarantee on sets of ≤ 6 offsets ([E4](../experiments/E4.md)); the table needed grows with N (2026-10-26). The two-pick class holds on C29's family {2..N} at deficit 1 to N = 28 in both roles ([E14](../experiments/E14.md)); D ≤ 1 pick, or the cap m, fail from N = 7 even in one role (journal 2026-10-26: two-pick class on {3..N}).
- **Evidence.** Top picks are needed only on high sets and in endgames (journal 2026-10-26: while the set is low, the defender needs only its smallest numbers). Turns of ≤ 2 picks for both players suffice to N = 24 ([E10](../experiments/E10.md)). On high sets every turn is one pick (C13) and Hyb2's potential W never rises (C25, Lean-checked).
- **Kill test.** The needed k grows without bound with N at every threshold; or the switch positions D can force admit no class H on which D draws (e.g. Hyb2 or any high strategy loses from positions D cannot avoid).
