# I32 Temperature of the low phase

Thermographs of Catch-Up positions: the low phase hot, the high phase cold, the mean of every even-total interval 0. Technique `T816` temperature theory. Source: user's toolbox sweep, 2026-11-05.

**Kill test.** Thermographs of the positions up to N ≈ 14: is the mean of every even-total interval 0, and does the temperature mark the low phase's boundary? Done (scratch `uni/thermo.rs`, `Param`): cooling a position by t taxes every pick by t, and since the turn rule compares the taxed scores, that is the real-height game (C32) at height 1 − t; the thermograph of a tied interval is its value over the height. For N ≡ 0 mod 4 it is flat, a draw at every h > 0 (N = 4, 8, 12 here; C109 to k = 7, E37). For N ≡ 3 mod 4 the count is odd, so the margin carries ±h and the tied value swings between W and L on open intervals, drawing only at isolated heights among them h = 1 (N = 7: D at 1, 3, 5; N = 11: D at 1/3, 1, 3, 5, 7, 9); its last breakpoint (h = 5 at N = 7, 9 at N = 11, about N − 2) marks where the rounds regime takes over.
**Status.** dead as a new route: the thermographs are C32's height functions, already computed; the mean is the rounds regime's value (0 at even totals, C13), and a descent through temperature is C88's descent in height, refuted at non-integer heights.
