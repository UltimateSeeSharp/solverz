# I1 Induction on intervals by peeling pairs

- **Reduction.** If adding a pair {x, x+1} above every remaining number changes a position's value by a fixed rule of its values at gaps g − 1, g, g + 1, then the values on {1..N} follow from those on {1..N−2}, and the conjecture reduces to small cases. Root claim: C1.
- **Metric.** Largest universe U (positions with numbers from {1..U}) on which the pair rule holds with no counterexample. Now: fails at U = 2, for windows up to g ± 4, even with x and parity in the key (2026-10-22).
- **Evidence.** Top twins {x, x} do cancel (mirror strategy), but a pair's effect depends on R globally (journal 2026-10-22: a top pair does not act locally).
- **Kill criteria.** A counterexample to the pair rule that no extra position feature repairs. Met: C42.

**Status.** `dead`: C42
