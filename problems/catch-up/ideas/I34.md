# I34 Universal catch-up rules

C1 is one member of a family of rules, the threshold-θ variants, that all draw at even totals from some N₀(θ) on (C120, I31's data); caps on turn length and asymmetric rules draw too. A proof that works for every θ at once has to rest on what the rules share (the player behind moves, at least two numbers per turn) and cannot use the level law or the exact tie rule, which removes a whole class of tools and points at the ones that remain (potentials of the remaining set and the deficit, LG-like bounds). So the low column (C104, I23) and the dense sets (C99, I20) are the rule-robust families containing C1, and the medium-height bands (C86, C110) are specific to Catch-Up's rule; a uniform proof should aim at low-height families.
But play cannot stay there (`short/robust.rs`): from {1..N}, with the defender choosing among its optimal turns one that leaves a low (min ≤ |S|/2) or dense (holes ≤ |S|/3) set, it is still forced into positions with 7–8 numbers left that are neither from N = 15 (9 attacker nodes at N = 15, 28 at N = 16, e.g. ({6, 7, 10, 11, 12, 14, 15}, 3)). So play from C1 crosses the medium-height region, where values depend on the rule, and a proof for every θ at once would have to handle each rule's medium positions.
