# I34 Universal catch-up rules

C1 is one member of a family of rules, the threshold-θ variants, that all draw at even totals from some N₀(θ) on (C120, I31's data); caps on turn length and asymmetric rules draw too. A proof that works for every θ at once has to rest on what the rules share (the player behind moves, at least two numbers per turn) and cannot use the level law or the exact tie rule, which removes a whole class of tools and points at the ones that remain (potentials of the remaining set and the deficit, LG-like bounds). Technique `T301` generalize. Source: us, from the user's `T1011` sweep (I31), 2026-11-05.

**Kill test.** N₀(θ) at more θ and N (θ = 6..10 at N = 24, 27, 28; overnight, 1.3 GB at N = 24 with `vararr.rs`): a θ whose non-draws persist with no sign of stopping, or whose N₀ grows so fast that no uniform argument can cover it, makes the generalization useless as a proof target.
