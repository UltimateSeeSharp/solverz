# I29 Complement mirror

On {1..N} the defender answers the attacker's turn with the mirrors N + 1 − x of the attacker's numbers x (pairs summing to N + 1), as the twin mirror does below height 1 (C63). Technique `T809` mirror strategy. Source: user's toolbox sweep, 2026-11-05.

**Kill test.** The mirror rule against perfect play on {1..N}, N ≤ 24 (both roles; where the mirror cannot be played the defender plays freely, counted); if it fails, whether it holds from the high phase on (blocks at height ≥ 2p − 1, the mirror a + b − x), with Hyb2.
Kill test done (scratch `scratch/a19/mirror/mirror.rs`, 2026-11-05): on {1..N} the mirror defender (mirrors taken in increasing or decreasing order, free play only where no mirror turn exists) loses from N = 4 when the attacker opens, by about N (Y's margin 2, 6, 8, 10, 12, 14, 16 at N = 4..16), and the mirror opener loses from N = 7. In the high phase it fails too: on blocks {a..a + 2p − 1} with the mirror a + b − x, at a = 2p − 1 and at the rounds height (2p)², every band entry is lost by the mirror defender (p = 2..6; e.g. p = 6 at a = 144: −10 when the attacker opens), where C24's Hyb2 draws. Mirror pairs have matching cost about p², adjacent pairs p (C20's slack).
**Status.** dead: the complement mirror loses at every N ≥ 4 and on high blocks.
