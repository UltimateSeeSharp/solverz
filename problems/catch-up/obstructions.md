# Obstructions

- **O1 Induction by four on drawn sets** (log 2026-10-22): {2,7,9,14} is a draw, {2,7,9,14} ∪ {15,16,17,18} a player-1 win. Rules out: proving {1..N} → {1..N+4} from "S is an even-sum draw" alone. Adding the four below fails too (ideator 2026-10-26, 19 sets within {1..22}, none within {1..18}): {12,13,15,19,21} is a draw, {1..4} ∪ {12,13,15,19,21} a player-1 win by opening 12 (`set_value`, both engines).
- **O2 Induction by four on complete sets** (same entry): {1,2,4,8,15,17,18,20,25} ∪ {26,27,28,29} is a player-1 win though the smaller set draws. Rules out: completeness as the induction invariant.
- **O3 Complete even-sum sets are draws** (same entry): first exception {1,2,3,7,9,11,14,15,22} (player 1 wins by opening 2); none with max ≤ 21. Rules out: completeness alone as the characterization of draws.
- **O4 A top pair acts locally on gaps** (log 2026-10-22: a top pair does not act locally): R = {} and {1..7} agree on V(R, 0..1) but V({1,2}, 0) = W, V({1..9}, 0) = L; windows up to w = 4, even with x and parity in the key, leave most keys undetermined. Rules out: peeling {x, x+1} off the top by a local rule on rows (A1). Margins do not help (log 2026-10-28: answering the quadruple at once loses; margin rows are not local): S = {1,2,3,4,5,9} and {1,2,4,8,9} have the same full margin row and sum, yet with {13..16} on top, at d = 7, one is a mover win and the other a loss (refuter).
- **O5 Fixed hole bounds under drawing play** (log 2026-10-22: holes grow under drawing play): for holes below max, internal holes, broken adjacent pairs and broken mirror pairs, and every fixed bound h, the attacker can force more than h holes at a defender's turn start with c(h) numbers left, c(h) growing with N (adjacent pairs, h = 2: 0, 6, 8, 11 left at N = 12, 16, 20, 24). Rules out: an A2 class "at most h holes" with fixed h plus a bounded endgame.
- **O6 Forced partner reply** (log 2026-10-22: answering with the partner is not a strategy): a defender that starts every turn with the adjacent partner of the attacker's last pick, and plays perfectly otherwise, loses from N = 11 (as player 1) and N = 12, 15, 16 (both sides) to an attacker that never blunders; e.g. N = 11: at R = {4,5,7,8,9}, g = 3, one broken pair, the forced 7 loses and only 4 draws. Rules out: a pairing strategy in which the partner reply is unconditional.
- **O7 Pair sets draw** (log 2026-10-22: pair sets do not all draw): C = {17,18,20,21,25,26,28,29,31,32} has V(C, 1) = loss, and {1,2} ∪ C is a player-1 win (opening 2); 232 exceptions with p = 5 and max ≤ 44, all with a sparse high part; none found with p ≤ 4. Rules out: L8 as stated, so proving L1 through "every pair set draws at g0" (A5); any argument that uses only the pair structure and not density. Aligned pair sets (pairs {2j−1, 2j}, the sets partner exchanges reach from {1..N}) fail too: {21,22,25,26,31,32,35,36,39,40} at gap 1 is a loss (log 2026-10-22: aligned pair sets do not all draw either).
- **O8 Opening the top quadruple early never helps** (log 2026-10-22: the top quadruple can wait, but not by a local lemma): at positions of {1..N} with T = {N−3..N} intact, letting the attacker open a T-pair early changes the value at about 30% of them (7.4% / 58.1% of positions reachable in Γ_24, by side to move), from N = 8 on at reachable positions. Rules out: Lemma A as a position-wise statement, so A6's split into "early openings never help" and "T played last"; L9's (P2) for dense sets.
- **O9 A block's drawn band never shrinks as its height decreases** (log 2026-10-22: even blocks draw at every height, but the band can shrink): {3,4,5,6} draws at gap 2, {2,3,4,5} is a mover win there. Rules out: A7's descent in a by "the band always contains every draw-parity gap ≤ p".
- **O10 The mover at a tie never loses the rounds game** (log 2026-10-26: rounds game tables to 24 offsets): R({0,4,5,6,7,8,10,11}, 0) = loss (best margin −1); 22,992 of the 4.2M even sets within {0..23}, none within {0..10}. Rules out: an induction over all offset sets through "the first picker at a tie is safe" or "the mover never ends worse than its debt" (μ(O, d) ≥ −d); a proof of T1 or T2 must use the structure of intervals or pair sets. In the real game the same sets are player-2 wins once placed high, e.g. {75,80,81,84,86,88,89,91} (even total 674, every opening loses; refuter's find, re-checked with `set_value` and `reference_value`), so even-total sets of distinct numbers can lose for player 1.
- **O11 L23's depth class on {1..N}** (log 2026-10-26: the depth class does not transplant to low sets): with q = half the numbers left, player 2's first turn start after any opening x ≥ 2 has deficit x > q − D_Z, for every even-total N ≤ 20 (q rounded either way). Rules out: K's inequality "deficit ≤ q − D_Z(numbers left)" as A2's class at height 1; the deficit is measured in numbers, not offsets, so a transplant needs a rescaled deficit.
- **O12 The level law off ties** (log 2026-10-26: L29's family holds in the two-pick class to N = 28): {3,4,5} with the mover trailing by 2 has a drawing pick and a winning pick; the trichotomy fails at 13,374 of 65,519 positions with 0 < d < min (review ideator, `scratch/ideator/review3/trich.rs`). Rules out: copying lemmas (L26, L28, L30) at positions that are not exact ties.
- **O13 Values monotone in the deficit** (same entry): {2,3} is a draw for the mover trailing by 1 and a win trailing by 3; V(R, d + 1) > V(R, d) at 2,847 (R, d) within {1..10}. Rules out: induction on the deficit with a monotone value, and "a bigger lead is always safer" classes (also `cap_mono.rs`, review ideator).
- **O14 Debt thresholds on high sets** (log 2026-10-26: high-set safety has no threshold structure in the debt): in the rounds game the mover's safe debts on {0,2,3,5} are 0 and 4, not 2 (exact values; gaps in 84 of 512 sets within {0..10}); under Hyb2T gaps in 175 of 512. Rules out: describing A8's hand-over class H, or high-set rows in general, by one debt threshold per offset set.
- **O15 Symmetric sets draw** (log 2026-10-26: review ideator: a halving induction): S = {1,6,8,9,14,15,17,22} (S = 23 − S, even sum) is a win for the mover at a tie, only by opening 1 (re-solved with an independent memoized solver; the ideator: every symmetric set with M ≤ 21 draws). Rules out: arguments that use only the reflection x ↦ M − x.
- **O16 Escalation answers** (log 2026-10-26: escalation needs free play: no answer rule, no return to R): at R_N's escalation positions no answer rule draws everywhere (32 positions at N = 20 where neither h − 1/h + 1 nor Hyb2 on the top half draws); answers inside the top half lose at 7 positions at N = 24 ({2,9,12} ∪ {14..24} after E took 13, D trailing by 4: only 2, 9, 12 draw); after one free exchange the low game's isolated-optimal moves fail from N = 15. Rules out: A9's escalation lemma as a fixed answer, an answer in the top half, or one exchange that returns play to R.
- **O17 One strategy for all heights** (log 2026-10-26: no drawing strategy works across all heights): on B(h, p) with h unknown to the players (only play reveals it), no player has one drawing strategy for every real h once p ≥ 3 (P1, p odd) or p ≥ 4; the fewest h-ranges grow with p (P1: 2, 2, 3, 4, 5 for p = 3..7), split at integers. Rules out: proving L32 or L12 with one strategy uniform in the height.
- **O18 A defender that closes the moved quadruple at once** (log 2026-10-28: answering the quadruple at once loses): on {1..N−4} ∪ ({N−3..N} + c), a defender that takes from the quadruple only to close a pair the attacker opened, as the first pick of its next turn while low numbers remain, loses in both roles at N = 7, 8, 11, 12, 15, 16 for every c tested (0 to 1000); at c = 0, D second: A 1, D 2, A 4, D must take 5 (unrestricted it answers 3). Closing within the next turn after low grabs fails too (N = 12 at c ≤ 1, N = 15, 16 at c ≤ 2; refuter). Rules out: proving L35 by a defender whose game tree does not depend on c (A6's reservation with immediate partner answers).
