# Obstructions

- **O1 Induction by four on drawn sets** (log 2026-10-22): {2,7,9,14} is a draw, {2,7,9,14} ∪ {15,16,17,18} a player-1 win. Rules out: proving {1..N} → {1..N+4} from "S is an even-sum draw" alone.
- **O2 Induction by four on complete sets** (same entry): {1,2,4,8,15,17,18,20,25} ∪ {26,27,28,29} is a player-1 win though the smaller set draws. Rules out: completeness as the induction invariant.
- **O3 Complete even-sum sets are draws** (same entry): first exception {1,2,3,7,9,11,14,15,22} (player 1 wins by opening 2); none with max ≤ 21. Rules out: completeness alone as the characterization of draws.
- **O4 A top pair acts locally on gaps** (log 2026-10-22: a top pair does not act locally): R = {} and {1..7} agree on V(R, 0..1) but V({1,2}, 0) = W, V({1..9}, 0) = L; windows up to w = 4, even with x and parity in the key, leave most keys undetermined. Rules out: peeling {x, x+1} off the top by a local rule on rows (A1).
- **O5 Fixed hole bounds under drawing play** (log 2026-10-22: holes grow under drawing play): for holes below max, internal holes, broken adjacent pairs and broken mirror pairs, and every fixed bound h, the attacker can force more than h holes at a defender's turn start with c(h) numbers left, c(h) growing with N (adjacent pairs, h = 2: 0, 6, 8, 11 left at N = 12, 16, 20, 24). Rules out: an A2 class "at most h holes" with fixed h plus a bounded endgame.
- **O6 Forced partner reply** (log 2026-10-22: answering with the partner is not a strategy): a defender that starts every turn with the adjacent partner of the attacker's last pick, and plays perfectly otherwise, loses from N = 11 (as player 1) and N = 12, 15, 16 (both sides) to an attacker that never blunders; e.g. N = 11: at R = {4,5,7,8,9}, g = 3, one broken pair, the forced 7 loses and only 4 draws. Rules out: a pairing strategy in which the partner reply is unconditional.
