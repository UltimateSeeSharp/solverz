# C12 NL family: NL_{2^m}(2^(m−1)+1) PDS for every m

For every m ≥ 3, C₂^(2m) has a partial difference set of negative Latin square type
NL_n(n/2 + 1), n = 2^m. With C11 this gives an SDS (n², (n+1)(n/2+1) + 1, (n/2+1)(n/2+2)) for
every m.

**Evidence.** Open. Members (`scratch/refuter/nl-family/report.md`): m = 3 (hyperoval complement, O28; also in
C₂² × C₄² and C₈², O29, O30), m = 4 (three order-5 classes of GF(256), O32), m = 5 (Brouwer's
SRG table, (1024,462,206,210), Dissett's 4-ary code; set not recomputed), m = 6 (30 norm
classes of GF(2¹²), O33, new). For m ≥ 7 no source, no construction and no non-existence
result; the semiprimitive cyclotomic mechanism works only at m = 4. Unopened: Polhill DCC
2008, Hamilton 2002, Cossidente–Pavese 2014, Bamberg et al. 2007, Dissett's thesis.
**Tried.** Cyclotomic searches at m = 7 (N = 381) and m = 8 (N = 85, 255): no hit.
