# C10 Multiplier-fixed coset sums on a quotient (A7)

Under C9, for a quotient H with gcd(|H|, n) = 1 the coset sums a on H (C3) may be taken
fixed by M; so no SDS exists when no M-fixed vector meets C3 and C4 on some such H.

**Evidence.** Refuter SURVIVES ([R5](../reviews/R5.md)): the proof needs no ±1 coefficients (Tan's Result
3.2 covers integer vectors on H); all 232 kills of the ideator's partial run reproduced with an
independent search validated on 317 quotients of known sets. The run stopped at 2,426 of 9,924
Instances.
**Tried.** —
**Proof.** C9 applied to the image of D in Z[H]. Lean: every n ≠ 0 prime to |H|, `IsSDS.exists_translate_fixed_contract'` (`MultiplierWitt.lean`);
refuter SURVIVES ([R26](../reviews/R26.md)).
