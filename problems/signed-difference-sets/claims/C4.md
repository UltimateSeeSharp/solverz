# C4 Coset sums: absolute sum and parity

For an SDS D with parameters (v, k, λ) on G and a homomorphism φ : G → H, the coset sums
a(h) = Σ_{φ(x) = h} D(x) satisfy Σ_h |a(h)| ≤ k, and k − Σ_h |a(h)| is even. For φ
surjective every fibre has |G|/|H| elements.

**Used by.** E5 and Lean's `noVector` (pruning and the leaf test), through
`not_hasSDS_of_noVector`.
**Evidence.** Each a(h) is a sum of ±1 over the marked elements of its fibre, so
|a(h)| ≤ their number m(h) ≡ a(h) mod 2, and Σ_h m(h) = k. E5's validation: no existing
Instance with v ≤ 40 is killed.
**Proof.** As in the evidence; the fibres of a surjective φ are cosets of its kernel. Lean:
`IsSDS.sum_abs_contract_le`, `IsSDS.sum_abs_contract_parity`, `card_fibre_eq` in
`lean/SignedDifferenceSets/Contraction.lean`. Refuter pending.
