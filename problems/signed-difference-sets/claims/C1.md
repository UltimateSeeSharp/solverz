# C1 Square condition

For an SDS D with parameters (v, k, λ): (Σₓ D(x))² = k + λ(v − 1). So
k + λ(v − 1) is a perfect square s².

**Used by.** Every search: D has (k + s)/2 or (k − s)/2 plus signs. C13, C14 (sign sum ±s).
**Evidence.** The trivial character applied to D D⁻¹ = (k − λ) + λG (Gordon,
arXiv:2212.10630); O1–O5 satisfy it.
**Proof.** Sum Σₓ D(x)D(x + g) over all g, the term g = 0 included: the left
side is (Σ D)², the right side k + λ(v − 1). Lean: `IsSDS.sum_sq`, `HasSDS.isSquare`,
`not_hasSDS_of_not_isSquare` in `lean/SignedDifferenceSets/Square.lean`; refuter SURVIVES
(`scratch/refuter/c1-c8-lean/C1-C8-2026-11-06-1615.md`).
