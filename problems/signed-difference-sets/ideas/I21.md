# I21 Doubling: an SDS on C₂ × K from a halved SDS and a weighing matrix (exists)

SDS (v, k, λ) on C₂ × K: under u ↦ 1 and u ↦ −1, DD* becomes 4ZZ* = n + 2λK and 4TT* = n, and ℚ[C₂ × K] is their product.
