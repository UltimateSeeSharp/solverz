# I21 Doubling: an SDS on C₂ × K from a halved SDS and a weighing matrix (exists)

The converse of C26 at r = 1: if Z is an SDS (v/2, (k + λ)/4, λ/2) on K and T a group weighing matrix of weight n/4 on K
(TT* = n/4) with supp T inside the zeros of Z, then D = P·Z + (1 − u)·T (D(0, x) = Z(x) + T(x), D(1, x) = Z(x) − T(x)) is an
SDS (v, k, λ) on C₂ × K: under u ↦ 1 and u ↦ −1, DD* becomes 4ZZ* = n + 2λK and 4TT* = n, and ℚ[C₂ × K] is their product.
Rows: open rows on C₂ × K with 4 ∣ n whose halved row exists (10 with r = 1; `scratch/c26/construct.py`). Found 2026-11-07:
O79 (N448, from O7) and O80 (N463, from the Paley-and-zero set in GF(27)); none inside the zeros of O8 (N449), O10 (N464)
or lift.py's (27, 17, 8) set (N469); other halved sets may still work. n/4 = 25 on K of order 125 (N16017, N16018,
N16152, N16153) needs a structured T, not a plain search.
