### Statement, restated
For an SDS D (v, k, λ) on a finite abelian G with n = k − λ ≠ 0 and gcd(v, n) = 1, one translate D(· + a) is fixed
by every t with gcd(t, v) = 1 and t ≡ p^f (mod exp G) for each prime p | n (f may depend on p) (C9'); the same for
the coset sums on a quotient H with gcd(|H|, n) = 1 (C10'). With it, each of 1,620 open rows (n not squarefree,
gcd(v, n) = 1) has no SDS because the orbit exhaust under one such t finds none (`not_hasSDS_of_orbitRow'`).
Reading tested: the plain one; no ambiguity found (t ≡ p^f mod e ⇔ ∀x, t•x = p^f•x).
