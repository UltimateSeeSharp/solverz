### Part 1. `not_hasSDS_of_sylow` says C22's divisibility form, never more (SURVIVES)
Hypothesis by hypothesis (SylowTrace.lean 384-393), against claims/C22.md:
- **r and the quotient.** `hcert` is `CycloFacts p r`, false unless r is a power of p (round 5, Part 1), so the
  characters land in ℤ[ζ_{rp}] with rp = p^J. Factors are powers of p dividing rp, so H is any p-group quotient of
  exponent ≤ p^J; r larger than exp(H)/p is allowed and harmless (the pairing scales by rp/d; `exists_pair_eq` still
  finds ⟨u, h⟩ = r, an element of order p, for every h ≠ 0, so orthogonality holds).
- **q against ⌈e/2⌉.** 2(qφ+1) ≤ eφ gives: p odd (φ ≥ 2 even): q+1 ≤ ⌈e/2⌉, equality reachable, = C22. p = 2, r = 1
  (φ = 1): q+1 ≤ ⌊e/2⌋. p = 2, r ≥ 2: q+1 ≤ ⌈e/2⌉; for e odd this exceeds C22's 2-part, but C22 (and He–Wu Thm 4.2,
  or simply a real character of order 2: χ(D)² = n) already says no SDS exists when v is even and n is not a square, so
  it is vacuous there. e even = 2w: q+1 ≤ w = C22's 2^min(t,w). The p = 2 equality (2^(w+1) ∤ s) is not in the Lean
  form; it is weaker than C22, not stronger.
- **Degenerate inputs.** n = k − λ = 0 (C22 needs n > 0): p^e ∣ 0 for all e, so the theorem gives p^t ∣ s; true,
  since χ(Y)·χ̄(Y) = 0 in a domain makes Y constant on H. n < 0: no SDS on G ≠ 1 at all. s = 0: never kills. t = 0
  or ds = []: p^0 = 1, never kills. ℕ subtraction p − 1: p prime ≥ 2. min is ℕ min. s is any root of s² = k + λ(v−1),
  sign irrelevant. Fintype instances arbitrary (card is instance-free). The conclusion is the problem's own `HasSDS`
  (Statement.lean 25-32: |supp D| = k, every non-zero shift correlates to λ).

### Part 2. `SyCert.Kills` against the theorem: every hypothesis derived in the kernel-checked `SyCert.not_hasSDS` (SURVIVES)
SylowCheck.lean 44-66:
- **Prime.** `IsPrimeTD p` (trial division, `IsPrimeTD.prime`) gives `Fact p.Prime`; `0 < r` gives `NeZero (r p)`.
- **Quotient.** `Dvds ds ms` (dᵢ ∣ mᵢ, same length, Rows.lean 62-65) gives `quot`, surjective by `quot_surjective`;
  `∀ m ∈ ms, 0 < m` gives the finite `Grp ds`.
- **PPowersB.** `0 < d ∧ d ∣ r p ∧ p ^ Nat.log p d = d`: for p ≥ 2 and d > 0, p^⌊log_p d⌋ = d iff d is a power of p
  (d = 1 → log 0). Given (p, r) ∈ maPairs, d ∣ rp already forces a p-power, so this conjunct is redundant, not weak.
- **Card relation.** |Grp ms| = (ms.prod / ds.prod) · |Grp ds| by `Nat.div_mul_cancel` from `Dvds.prod_dvd`: exact,
  no floor-division junk. `ds.prod = p^t` gives `ht`; `s² = k + λ(ms.prod − 1)` is rewritten to the theorem's
  `k + λ(c |H| − 1)` (ms.prod − 1 is in ℤ, no ℕ truncation).
- **Pass-through.** `p^e ∣ k − λ` (in ℤ), the q inequality and `¬ p^min(t, q+1) ∣ s` are passed unchanged.
- **Cyclotomic facts.** `(p, r) ∈ maPairs` → `MaRows.cycloFacts_of_mem_maPairs` (25 certified pairs, round 5); the
  rows use 9 of them, all with r a power of p.
- **Not gameable.** A wrong Kills could only make `decide +kernel` fail, not prove a false row. Kills is not trivially
  true: controls in R6Control.lean (see Part 4).
