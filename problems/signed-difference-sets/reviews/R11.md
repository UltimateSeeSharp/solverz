## Findings

Restatement.
- C1 (`Square.lean`): for any SDS D in a finite abelian G with k marks and off-diagonal
  autocorrelation λ, (Σ D)² = k + λ(|G| − 1) over ℤ; so HasSDS ⇒ IsSquare(k + λ(v − 1)); contrapositive.
- C8 (`Parity.lean`): v = |G| odd, λ even, d = v − k (ℕ, truncated), d(d − 1) < v − 1 (ℕ) ⇒ ¬HasSDS.
- Rows: `Grp [m₁,…,m_r]` is ZMod m₁ × (… × ZMod m_r) with the product instances, so a per-row
  `¬ @HasSDS (Grp ms) _ inst k λ` is about the row's own group.
- Iso: HasSDS is invariant under `≃+` and under change of `Fintype` instance.
