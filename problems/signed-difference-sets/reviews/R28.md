## Findings

Restatement. D: G -> {0, +-1}, |supp D| = k, D D^(-1) = n + lam G, n = k - lam, 4 | n. For a surjection
phi: G -> H = C_{2^j} x K (|K| odd, 2^i = -1 on K), Y = coset sums of D, c = |G| / |H|, u = (2^(j-1), 0):
(a) 2 | Y(h + u) - Y(h) for all h; (b) 8 | 2 s' - (k + lam (2c - 1)), s' = sum D = +-s.
At c = 1: supp D is <u>-invariant and (D + D(. + u))/2 on G/<u> is an SDS (v/2, (k + lam)/4, lam/2).
Unambiguous; (b) follows from (a) by elementary algebra, (a) is Ma's lemma with b = 1.
