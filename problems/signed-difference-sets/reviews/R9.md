## Findings

Claim under attack (other session's hand-off, `scratch/other-session/handoff-sds.md`):
- Lemma: P a PDS (v, k_P, λ', μ) in abelian G, P = P^(−1), 0 ∉ P, λ' − μ = 2. Then D = P − {0}
  (P marked +1, identity −1) is an SDS (v, k_P + 1, μ).
- Family: NL_n(r) has λ' − μ = 2r − n, so r = n/2 + 1; with n = 2^m an NL_n(n/2+1) PDS in C2^(2m)
  gives SDS (n², (n+1)(n/2+1)+1, (n/2+1)(n/2+2)). m = 3: N735 (64,46,30); m = 4: N17201 (256,154,90).
- Restated: D = P − 1 in Z[G]; claim D D^(−1) = (k_D − μ) + μ G with k_D = |supp D| = k_P + 1.
  Unambiguous; the Lean `IsSDS k lam D` (support size k, every g ≠ 0 has signed count lam)
  is exactly this coefficientwise.

### F1. Lemma: holds (one-line identity, all cases)
(P − 1)(P^(−1) − 1) = P² − 2P + 1 (uses P = P^(−1)); P² = (k_P − μ) + (λ' − μ)P + μG, so
D D^(−1) = (k_P − μ + 1) + (λ' − μ − 2)P + μG. With λ' − μ = 2 this is (k_D − μ) + μG, k_D = k_P + 1
(0 ∉ P, so the support is P ∪ {0}). Degenerate PDS (P = ∅, P = G − 1, where μ resp. λ' is not
determined by P) still satisfy the identity formally; nothing is lost. Abelian is not even needed
beyond P = P^(−1) and identity central. Signed version of the folklore fact (from memory; Ma's 1994 survey not opened) that a
PDS with λ' = μ − 2 plus the identity is a difference set: (P + ε)² has P-coefficient λ' − μ + 2ε.
Numerically: a 2-class NL_16(6) (λ' − μ = −4) and P + 1 instead of P − 1 both fail as predicted.

### F2. NL parameter algebra: correct
NL_n(r) = (n², r(n+1), −n + r² + 3r, r² + r) satisfies k(k − λ − 1) = (v − k − 1)μ, λ − μ = 2r − n,
complement NL_n(n − 1 − r); r = n/2 + 1 gives SDS (n², (n+1)(n/2+1) + 1, (n/2+1)(n/2+2)) and
(Σ D)² = k + λ(v − 1) holds (checked n = 4..128, `verify_sets.py` companion inline check).
n = 8: (64,46,30); n = 16: (256,154,90); n = 32: (1024,562,306), outside the table (max v there: 883).

### F6. Why a uniform cyclotomic family stops at m = 4 (own argument + computation)
- Semiprimitive cyclotomy (the N17201 mechanism): t classes of order N in GF(2^(2m)) have
  r = t(n − 1)/N. r = n/2 + 1 needs (n − 1)/g | N with g = gcd(2^m − 1, 2^(m−1) + 1) | 3 (g = 3 iff
  m even). Every divisor > 2 of a semiprimitive N is semiprimitive, and 2^m − 1 (m odd ≥ 3) and
  (2^m − 1)/3 (m even ≥ 6) are not (2^j ≢ −1). So this mechanism gives the family only at m = 4
  (N = 5, t = 3), plus the trivial m = 2.
- Field reduction of i-ovoids of W(2r − 1, 2^e) gives NL_n(i(2^e − 1)) (i-ovoids of W(2m−1, q) are
  two-character sets: Bamberg–Kelly–Law–Penttila 2007, cited by Feng–Wang–Xiang, not opened); 2^e − 1 | n/2 + 1 only for
  e ≤ 2, and from W(3, 2^e)-ovoid partitions (Ebert / cyclotomy) only n = 16 hits r = n/2 + 1.
- Exhaustive search (`cyc/`, Rust, validated: GF(64), N = 21, t = 15 returns exactly the 120
  hyperoval complements containing a fixed point = 168·15/21; GF(256), N = 5 returns all C(4,2) unions;
  GF(1024), N = 31, t = 15 returns exactly the 15 trace-hyperplane quadrics):
  GF(1024), N = 31 (norm circles over GF(32), GF(4)C_11-invariant), t = 14: 0 hits of 1.2·10^8
  (`m5_N31_t14.out`); Frobenius-invariant unions for N = 93: 0 hits. So the m = 5 set of F5 is not
  a cyclotomic union of these shapes. m = 6 runs: see F8.

### F10. Literature on existence of NL_{2^m}(2^(m−1) ± ...) for general m (opened 2026-11-06)
Known members (r = n/2 + 1, equivalently complement r = n/2 − 2), all in C2^(2m):
m = 3 hyperoval T2*(O) (classical; also Bao–Xiang–Zhao arXiv:2407.15632 abstract, Denniston
parameters q = 2, m = 2, r = 1); m = 4 semiprimitive cyclotomy; m = 5 Dissett's 4-ary [154,5] code
(Brouwer table, F5); m = 6 our F8 (no source found). No source found for any m ≥ 7, nor a general
construction, nor a nonexistence result. Opened and checked, none gives r = n/2 + 1 or n/2 − 2 in
general:
- Momihara–Xiang, arXiv:1212.5752 (PDF text read): Thm 3.1 lifts cyclotomic SRGs on F_{q^m} to NL
  sets in F_{q^{2m}}; corollaries give r = (q^m − 1)/e (semiprimitive) and r = q^(m−a) − 1 (subfield;
  q = 2, a = 1 is the quadric r = n/2 − 1). Our m = 6 set T is not a union of cosets of any subgroup
  of GF(64)*, so it is not one of their corollaries.
- Momihara–Wang–Xiang survey arXiv:1809.03007 (HTML): no char-2 NL example with N = 63 or r = 30.
- Feng–Wang–Xiang arXiv:1909.07954 (ar5iv): m-ovoids of W(2r−1, q) from cyclotomic SRGs, p odd only;
  i-ovoids of W(2m−1, 2) would give NL_{2^m}(i) (two-character), the family needs i = 2^(m−1) − 2.
- Ceria–Pavese arXiv:2207.01128 (abstract): W(5,2) has m-ovoids only for m = 3. So N735's P (r = 5)
  and its complement (r = 2) are not m-ovoids: the family is not an m-ovoid family in general.
- Davis–Xiang arXiv:math/0407423 (PDF text): NL in Z4^(2k) × Z2^(4ℓ−4k) with quadric-type r only.
- Sánchez Menchén et al. arXiv:2607.19842 (HTML): NL in five non-elementary groups, r = 42, 170, 682,
