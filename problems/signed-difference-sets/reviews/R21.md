### 1. Proof: every step holds (by hand; intermediate claims also checked numerically, finding 2)
- ψ(a)σψ(a) = n for ψ ≠ 1 from aa⁽⁻¹⁾ = n + λ(v/p^t)H (C3), ψ(H) = 0. OK.
- (p) = (π)^Φ, π = ζ − 1, Φ = φ(p^J); σπ = −ζ⁻¹π ⇒ v_π(σx) = v_π(x) ⇒ v_π(ψ(a)) = Φe/2 exactly. Φe is even: p odd ⇒ Φ
  even (so e odd is fine for p odd); p = 2, J ≥ 2 ⇒ Φ even; J = 1 ⇒ ψ(a) ∈ ℤ, ψ(a)² = n. Any nontrivial 2-group has an
  order-2 character, so n = m² whenever p = 2 | v.
- Characters of lower order p^j sit in ℤ[ζ_{p^j}] ⊂ ℤ[ζ_{p^J}], v_{π_J} = p^{J−j}v_{π_j}: all have v_{π_J} = Φe/2.
- z = p^t a₀ − s′ = Σ_{ψ≠1} ψ(a) ∈ ℤ, v_π(z) = Φ·v_p(z) ≥ Φe/2 ⇒ v_p(z) ≥ ⌈e/2⌉ ⇒ p^min(t,⌈e/2⌉) | s′ (both t < ⌈e/2⌉
  and t ≥ ⌈e/2⌉; s′ = 0 included).
- p = 2: 2^R − 1 real nontrivial characters (R = 2-rank of H ≥ 1), values ±m, sum mT, T odd, v₂ = w := v₂(m). Non-real:
  x = π^a y (a = Φw), x + σx = π^a(y + (−ζ⁻¹)^a σy), −1 ≡ ζ ≡ 1 and y ≡ σy mod π ⇒ π^(a+1) | x + σx (in the big ring,
  uniformly in the character's order). S_nr = 2^t a₀ − s′ − mT ∈ ℤ ⇒ v₂(S_nr) ≥ w + 1 ⇒ v₂(2^t a₀ − s′) = w exactly.
  Case t ≤ w: v₂(s′) < t would give v₂ = v₂(s′) < w. Case t > w: v₂(s′) < w gives v₂ = v₂(s′); v₂(s′) > w (incl. s′ = 0)
  gives v₂ ≥ min(t, v₂ s′) > w. Hence min(v₂ s, t) = min(w, t). The weakest step (p = 2 equality) holds.
- The Lean-friendly induction "∀x, π^(2b) | xσx ⇒ π^b | x" (peel π off x, never divide n) is sound.

### 5. For the Lean statement (not a flaw of C22): v_p(0) and e/2 junk values
s = 0 exactly on (v, v − 1, −1); 53 such rows are `exists`, all v = p^a odd with p | n (e = t = a), e.g. N1 (5,4,−1)
= O4, N2 (9,8,−1) in C₃² = O5 (both Lean-checked SDS), N1327 (81,80,−1). C22 holds there (p^min | 0), and both row
scripts treat v_p(0) as ∞. But Mathlib's `padicValNat p 0 = 0`, so a Lean form `min t ((e+1)/2) ≤ padicValNat p s`
(or `min (padicValNat 2 s) t = …`) is false on O4/O5 and unprovable. Use divisibility: p odd `p ^ min t ((e+1)/2) ∣ s`;
p = 2 (t ≥ 1) `n = m ^ 2 ∧ 2 ^ min t w ∣ s ∧ (w < t → ¬ 2 ^ (w+1) ∣ s)` with w = v₂(m). Likewise state n = m² before
using e/2 (ℕ division with e odd is junk; vacuous here since n = m² is forced).
