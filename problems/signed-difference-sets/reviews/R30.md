## Literature

- Miller, arXiv:2511.10231 (opened, html): Thm 7 = for every prime p | n₁, p self-conjugate mod v* or t ≡ p^j
  (mod the p-free part of v*); then t is a multiplier if n₁/gcd(n₁, v) > λ or a gcd(v, M̃) = 1 condition. Miller
  calls it a variant of Thm 3.1 of Gordon–Schmidt's survey and omits the proof.
- Gordon, Schmidt, "A survey of the multiplier conjecture" (2015-09-30 preprint, opened:
  personal.ntu.edu.sg/bernhard/Publications/pub/Multiplier_Survey.pdf): Thm 3.1 has exactly the alternative
  "(i) t ≡ u^f mod v* or (ii) u self-conjugate mod v*" for every prime u | n₁, generalizing Leung–Ma–Schmidt 2014
  Thm 1.4; its proof's case n = N (E E⁽⁻¹⁾ = 1 ⇒ E = ±g) is C25's argument. Self-conjugacy itself: Turyn (Result 2.3
  there). Neither has an even-valuation condition (for |H| > 1 an odd v_p(n) with p self-conjugate already forbids
  the SDS, since |χ(Y)|² = n for nontrivial χ; so the Lean's extra `p^(2j) ∥ n` loses nothing).
- Credit fix: C25.md credits only Miller Thm 7; the self-conjugate exemption is older: Gordon–Schmidt survey Thm 3.1
  (difference sets, from Leung–Ma–Schmidt 2014). C25's new part is the SDS / quotient-prime-to-n form.
- Gordon, Signed difference sets, arXiv:2212.10630 (opened, html): Thm 5.1 (McFarland's thesis via Tan 2018,
  Result 3.2): D D⁽⁻¹⁾ ≡ n mod G, gcd(v, n) = 1, t ≡ p_i^(f_i) mod e for EVERY prime p_i | n ⇒ some translate fixed:
  that is C10's form; no self-conjugate exemption. Search "multiplier theorem signed difference sets self-conjugate"
  (2026-11-07) found no SDS statement with the exemption, so the SDS form of C25 looks new; its ingredients are not.
