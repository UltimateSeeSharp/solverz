### Statement, restated
For each row (G = Grp ms, k, λ), with n = k − λ > 0: take a quotient H = Grp ds of G (|G| = c·|H|) with gcd(|H|, n) = 1
and t prime to |H| acting on H as p^f for every prime p | n. Then (C10, McFarland) some translate of the coset sums
a(h) = Σ_{φ(x)=h} D(x) is constant on the cycles of h ↦ t·h. Every coset-sum vector obeys |a| ≤ c, Σa = ±s,
Σa² = n + λc, Σ_h a(h)a(h+g) = λc (g ≠ 0), Σ|a| ≤ k with k − Σ|a| even, k ≤ Σ maxMarked(c, a). If no t-invariant
vector on H meets all of these, G has no SDS(k, λ). Each row theorem states `¬ HasSDS (Grp ms) k λ`.
Reading tested: the plain one. No ambiguity found.
