# Understanding

**Holds.**
- Gordon's table has 70,543 cases (`instances.csv`): 146 exist, 2,574 are
  ruled out by his searches, which covered cyclic groups; tiny non-cyclic
  cases stay Open, their cyclic twins "No, Orbit Exhaust" (README).
- He–Wu's four obstructions rule out 45,361 of the 67,823 Open cases on
  paper, and their constructions settle 3; 22,459 stay open (README,
  reproduced by the importer).
- "Open" in the table often means "never searched": P1 found sets for 16
  of the 41 open Instances with v ≤ 40 it finished (E4, E7). Non-cyclic
  groups hold most of them.
- "Open" is not enough for a Solution: the database lags the papers. N67 and
  N73 are Gordon 2022's Prop 2.3 and Thm 4.1; N61 is He–Chen–Ge's product
  construction at m = 1; N63319, N14604, N31832, N14471 are settled in those
  papers too (refuter, `scratch/refuter/r4-report.md`).
- Cyclotomic sign vectors of order 8 (P2) give SDSs only at 9 fields up to
  10⁵, no family (E6); one answers an open entry, N35840 (361, 316, 141) in
  C₁₉ × C₁₉, Lean-checked, refuter SURVIVES (O12).
- A found set is cheap to certify: `decide` checks a v = 27 set in about
  40 s; the bit-mask check `checkSet` (`decide +kernel`) a v = 361 set in
  77 s (N35840) (`Answer/N<id>`, generated by `solver/answer.py`).
- Non-existence by contraction (F3, F4) is now checked by the kernel:
  `noVector` mirrors the solver's search node for node (`Tests/Checks`), and
  `not_hasSDS_of_noVector` turns its `true` into `¬ HasSDS`. E5 finds 12 of
  the 46 open Instances with v ≤ 40 killed by a quotient; 11 build in Lean
  (seconds to 3 min each).
- The kernel keeps every reduction of one declaration: about 0.1 MB per
  search node (N79, 51,596 nodes: 7.5 GB). Larger searches must be split
  across declarations (`noVector_of_top`), and Lean modules must build one
  at a time (state.md, Resources).
**Blocks.** Non-existence counts only with a Lean proof. Contraction leaves
34 of the 46 open with v ≤ 40 undecided; exhaustion in the kernel runs about
300 P1 nodes per second, while E4 and E7's exhausted Instances need 10⁸ to
10¹⁰ nodes, so they need a stronger method (a lift above the contraction
vectors, more symmetry, or SAT proofs checked by Mathlib's `lrat_proof`).
**So a Solution must.** Per Instance: a set Lean checks, or a Lean proof that
none exists (exhaustion after F2, or contraction through `noVector`); and,
before it is proposed, a check against the known constructions of Gordon,
He–Chen–Ge and He–Wu.
