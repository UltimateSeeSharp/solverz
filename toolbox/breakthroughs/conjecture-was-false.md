# The conjecture was false
Many "obviously true" conjectures broke to an explicit object (S4–S7, S9; also S11, S22, S24), sometimes too subtle for sampling (S6); a disproof need not exhibit a witness (S8, S41). One widely doubted claim was true (S30).
Stuck after decades of failed proofs: test both truth values early.

### S4 Hirsch conjecture
Stuck: 1957–2010 · `find` · used: T1370, T838
- Unlock: spindles and a strong d-step theorem lift a 5-dimensional spindle of length 6 to a 43-dimensional counterexample with 86 facets.
- From: constructive polytope combinatorics.
- Source: [Wikipedia: Hirsch conjecture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hirsch_conjecture)

### S5 Kaplansky's unit conjecture
Stuck: 1940–2021 · `find` · used: T1001, T1, T1370
- Unlock: encode "a unit with support in a given set exists" as SAT over the Hantzsche–Wendt (Promislow) group over F₂; the unit has 21 group elements and a laptop sufficed. It came after two years of on-and-off attempts by a group theorist.
- From: SAT solving.
- Source: [Wikipedia: Kaplansky's conjectures](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kaplansky%27s_conjectures), [arXiv:2102.11818](https://arxiv.org/abs/2102.11818), [Münster](https://www.uni-muenster.de/MathematicsMuenster/de/news/artikel/2021-03-04.shtml), [Cambridge talk](https://talks.cam.ac.uk/talk/index/234613), [Simons](https://simons.berkeley.edu/news/solving-semidecidable-problems-group-theory)

### S6 Bunkbed conjecture
Stuck: mid-1980s–2024 · `find` · used: T1371, T1370, T1346
- Unlock: brute force (< 9 vertices) and neural-network search failed; Hollom's hypergraph counterexample, made robust, became a 7,222-vertex graph with a probability gap far too small to sample.
- From: a richer model (hypergraphs); the conjecture came from percolation (physicist Kasteleyn).
- Source: [Quanta](https://quantamagazine.org/maths-bunkbed-conjecture-has-been-debunked-20241101/), [arXiv:2410.02545](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.02545)

### S7 Hedetniemi's conjecture
Stuck: 1966–2019 · `find` · used: T1370, T840
- Unlock: Shitov's counterexample: χ(G × H) can be smaller than min(χ(G), χ(H)), built through exponential graphs.
- From: graph colouring; an "obviously true" categorical identity fell to one construction.
- Source: [Wikipedia: Hedetniemi's conjecture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hedetniemi%27s_conjecture), [arXiv:1905.02167](https://arxiv.org/abs/1905.02167)

### S8 Mertens conjecture
Stuck: 1885–1985 · `prove` · used: T46
- Unlock: Odlyzko–te Riele disproved it with zeta zeros and LLL, without an explicit counterexample; none is known, and the first lies below e^{3.21·10⁶⁴}. It was false despite massive computational support.
- From: lattice basis reduction.
- Source: [Wikipedia: Mertens conjecture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mertens_conjecture)

### S9 Strassen's algorithm
Stuck: to 1969 · `find` · used: T1370
- Unlock: seven multiplications for 2×2 blocks showed that n³ matrix multiplication is not optimal and opened the search for ω; the question is now attacked by flip graphs (T92) and AlphaTensor (T104).
- From: an explicit algorithm against an assumed lower bound.
- Source: [Wikipedia: Strassen algorithm](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Strassen_algorithm)
