# Physics predicts, mathematics proves
Heuristics gave exact answers years before proofs, and the prediction fixed the target (S48–S55); a table with gaps predicts its missing members the same way (S56). Physics often supplied the proof's tools too (S48, S50, S55).
Stuck without a target: get the exact predicted value first, by heuristic or numerics, then prove that value.

### S48 Monstrous moonshine
Stuck: 1978–1992 · `prove` · used: T1322, T841
- Unlock: McKay's 196884 = 196883 + 1; the moonshine vertex algebra (Frenkel–Lepowsky–Meurman); Borcherds' proof with the string-theory no-ghost theorem and generalized Kac–Moody algebras.
- From: string theory.
- Source: [Wikipedia: Monstrous moonshine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Monstrous_moonshine)

### S49 Counting rational curves on the quintic
Stuck: decades; physics 1991, proofs 1996 · `compute` · used: T1305, T1318
- Unlock: mirror symmetry predicted the counts (317,206,375 cubics); a disagreeing rigorous computation had a bug; Givental and Lian–Liu–Yau proved it.
- From: string theory, by physicists.
- Source: [Wikipedia: Mirror symmetry (string theory)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mirror_symmetry_(string_theory))

### S50 Witten's conjecture
Stuck: 1991–1992 · `prove` · used: T1307, T803
- Unlock: two models of 2D quantum gravity should agree, so intersection numbers satisfy KdV; Kontsevich proved it with ribbon graphs and a matrix integral.
- From: quantum gravity.
- Source: [Wikipedia: Witten conjecture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Witten_conjecture)

### S51 Parisi formula for the SK spin glass
Stuck: 1979–2006 · `bound` · used: T1304, T786
- Unlock: replica symmetry breaking predicted the free energy; Guerra's interpolation gave one bound, Talagrand the other.
- From: statistical physics.
- Source: [Wikipedia: Spin glass](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spin_glass), [Bolthausen](https://www.numdam.org/item/SB_2004-2005__47__349_0/)

### S52 Random k-SAT threshold
Stuck: physics prediction, proved for large k in 2014 · `bound` · used: T1304
- Unlock: the one-step replica symmetry breaking (1RSB) formula, proved by reducing moment calculations to tree recursions (Ding–Sly–Sun).
- From: statistical physics.
- Source: [arXiv:1411.0650](https://arxiv.org/abs/1411.0650)

### S53 Feigenbaum universality
Stuck: 1975–1982 · `compute` · used: T832, T1310
- Unlock: a numerically discovered universal constant, explained by a renormalization fixed point; Lanford's computer-assisted proof; Lyubich later without numerics.
- From: renormalization in physics.
- Source: [Wikipedia: Feigenbaum constants](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Feigenbaum_constants)

### S54 Conformal invariance of percolation
Stuck: physics prediction, proved 2001 · `prove` · used: T785
- Unlock: Smirnov linked critical percolation to SLE₆ and proved Cardy's formula; SLE also proved Mandelbrot's 4/3 conjecture.
- From: conformal field theory predictions.
- Source: [Wikipedia: Schramm–Loewner evolution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Schramm%E2%80%93Loewner_evolution)

### S55 Honeycomb self-avoiding walks
Stuck: 1982–2010 · `compute` · used: T785
- Unlock: Nienhuis's Coulomb-gas prediction μ = √(2+√2) was proved by Duminil-Copin–Smirnov with a parafermionic observable.
- From: physics predicts; a discrete holomorphic observable proves.
- Source: [arXiv:1007.0575](https://arxiv.org/abs/1007.0575), [Wikipedia: Self-avoiding walk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Self-avoiding_walk)

### S56 Mendeleev's predicted elements
Stuck: 1869–1886 · `find` · used: T1377
- Unlock: gaps in the periodic table predicted eka-aluminium, eka-boron and eka-silicon with their properties; gallium, scandium and germanium filled them. A classification table with gaps is a list of `find` targets with predicted invariants.
- From: chemistry.
- Source: [Wikipedia: Mendeleev's predicted elements](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mendeleev%27s_predicted_elements)
