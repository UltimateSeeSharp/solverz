# Failure as information, and a changed angle
A failure can say exactly what a working argument must do differently: the GPY level-of-distribution bottleneck (S16), Wiles's failed Kolyvagin–Flach step (S57), the parity problem and complexity barriers. Long attacks (in years: Huang ~7, S45; Marcus–Spielman–Srivastava 5, S30; Campos et al. 4, S17; Zhang 3, S16; Wiles 7, S57) ended with a changed angle, not more of the same (S16–S18).
Stuck after the same ideas keep failing: name what the failure demands, then change the angle (a neighbouring problem, a weaker tool that fits, another field's methods).

### S16 Bounded prime gaps
Stuck: 2005–2013 (twin primes since at least the 1840s) · `bound` · used: T1368, T1315, T507, T1355
- Unlock: GPY needed a level of distribution above 1/2; Bombieri–Friedlander–Iwaniec had one beyond 1/2 only in a form that did not fit GPY; Zhang used a less efficient sieve, restricted to numbers without large prime factors, flexible enough to take such an estimate (gap 70 million). Maynard independently refined the GPY weights (600); Polymath8 got 246.
- From: old sieve estimates made to fit; Zhang was a little-known lecturer who had worked as an accountant and in a sandwich shop.
- Source: [Quanta](https://www.quantamagazine.org/yitang-zhang-proves-landmark-theorem-in-distribution-of-prime-numbers-20130519/), [arXiv:1311.4600](https://arxiv.org/abs/1311.4600), [Wikipedia: Polymath Project](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polymath_Project)

### S17 Diagonal Ramsey upper bound
Stuck: 1935–2023 · `bound` · used: T428, T1367
- Unlock: grow a red "book" (clique spine plus pages) instead of a clique; the key step came after years stuck, by passing to off-diagonal R(k, l), which allowed a density increment (also a reformulation). R(k) < 3.993ᵏ.
- From: a refinement of Erdős–Szekeres plus Conlon's greedy idea.
- Source: [Quanta](https://www.quantamagazine.org/after-nearly-a-century-a-new-limit-for-patterns-in-graphs-20230502/), [arXiv:2303.09521](https://arxiv.org/abs/2303.09521)

### S18 Catalan's conjecture
Stuck: 1844–2002 · `prove` · used: T544, T324, T517
- Unlock: Tijdeman (1976) bounded all cases via Baker's transcendence method; Mihăilescu finished with cyclotomic fields and Galois modules.
- From: algebraic number theory after an analytic reduction.
- Source: [Wikipedia: Catalan's conjecture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Catalan%27s_conjecture)
