# Checking decides what counts
A claim counts once a check others can run accepts it. Formalization can referee frontier mathematics and teach the author something (S1); a proof experts cannot check does not count (S2); proofs of great size need systematic re-verification (S3).
Stuck on a step whose status is in doubt: check it formally before building on it.

### S1 Liquid Tensor Experiment
Stuck: 2020–2021 · `prove` · used: T150, T1346
- Unlock: Scholze asked for a Lean check of a Clausen–Scholze theorem he was unsure of; Commelin led a team that formalized it from a blueprint: the doubted part by June 2021, all of it by July 2022. Formalizing Gordan's lemma showed Scholze that the key step reduces a non-convex problem over the reals to a convex one over the integers.
- From: formal proof in Lean.
- Source: [Xena: Liquid tensor experiment](https://xenaproject.wordpress.com/2020/12/05/liquid-tensor-experiment/), [Xena: Half a year of the LTE](https://xenaproject.wordpress.com/2021/06/05/half-a-year-of-the-liquid-tensor-experiment-amazing-developments/), [Wikipedia: Condensed mathematics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Condensed_mathematics)
