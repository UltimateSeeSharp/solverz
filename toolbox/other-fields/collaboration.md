# Collaboration and shared testing
Working on a problem with others: open collaboration, co-designed decisive tests, blind benchmarks and search games for crowds.

### T1355 Open collaboration (Polymath)
`find` `prove` `bound` `compute` · stage: practice
aka: Polymath, massively collaborative mathematics, Gowers, blog, half-formed ideas, share early, specialised roles, Polymath5, Polymath8, Erdős discrepancy problem, bounded prime gaps
- What: many people post small, half-formed ideas fast in public; it draws on luck, diverse knowledge and specialised roles.
- Fits when: partial results exist; share them early, since reductions count even when the main problem stays open.
- Lean: —
- Example: [Gowers 2009](https://gowers.wordpress.com/2009/01/27/is-massively-collaborative-mathematics-possible/); Polymath5's reduction was used in Tao's EDP proof; Polymath8 reached gap 246 ([Wikipedia: Polymath Project](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polymath_Project)).
- See also: T18, T1341, T1374
