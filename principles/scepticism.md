# Scepticism

Signs to look closer, never reasons to reject:
- Too easy, or a sudden simplification: find where the difficulty went.
- Proves too much: would the same argument prove something false?
- Drift: it stops being about the problem.
- A missed case.
- The key new idea: what is it, and why did earlier attempts miss it?
- AI-written work: fluent is not evidence; a source counts once opened;
  Lean certifies the statement, not the prose.
