# Scepticism

## Red flags
- Too easy.
- Proves too much: would it also prove something false?
- A sudden simplification.
- Drift: it stops mentioning the problem.
- A twist on a popular method that experts supposedly missed.
- Endless reformulation.
- A contradiction from loose computation.
- A missed case: list every case, check each.
- Pieces harder than the whole.

## Before believing a result
- The key new idea; why earlier attempts fell short.
- Why it avoids the known obstructions.
- Its milestones and simplest consequence.
- Would one sign error destroy it?
- Did we try hard to disprove it?

## AI-written work
- Fluent is not evidence; errors look flawless.
- Never the only check of its own work.
- Cite only sources actually opened.
- A huge proof of a big result is almost surely wrong.
- Lean certifies the statement, not the informal proof.
